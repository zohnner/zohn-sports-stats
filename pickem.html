Confidence pool FAQ

What is a confidence pool?

You pick a winner for every game each week, then rank all your picks by confidence -- the highest-confidence pick is worth the most points, the lowest the fewest. Getting a high-confidence pick right matters more than getting a low-confidence one right.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free, no login, no ads.

Does it check multiple sportsbooks?

No -- it blends DraftKings' line with ESPN's own independent model instead, which gives you a real second opinion and flags exactly where the two disagree.

Can I change the ranking?

Yes -- drag any game to re-rank it or click a team to flip the pick. The default order is a starting point, not the final answer.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything saves locally in your own browser.
