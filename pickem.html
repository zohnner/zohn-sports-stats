Pick'em Confidence Helper

Default order blends DraftKings' line (moneyline, vig removed) with ESPN's own Matchup Predictor model — two independent signals, not multiple sportsbooks (ESPN only carries DraftKings' number). Games where the two disagree by a lot are flagged with an orange border — those are the ones worth your own judgment, not the safe chalk. Drag rows (or use the arrows) to reorder confidence points, click a team to flip the pick. Everything is saved locally per week in this browser only.
