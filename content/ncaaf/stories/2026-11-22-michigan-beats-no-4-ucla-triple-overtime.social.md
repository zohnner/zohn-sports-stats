**Title:** 6-5 Michigan ended No. 4 UCLA's unbeaten run 18-16 in triple overtime

UCLA was 10-0 and had not lost all season. Michigan tied it 10-10 in regulation, and both teams kicked a field goal in each of the 2 overtimes; then Michigan converted its 2-point try in the 3rd overtime while UCLA did not. Savion Hiter ran 26 times for 104 yards, and Bryce Underwood threw for 127 yards and a touchdown. Michigan is now 2-2 against ranked teams, with wins over No. 11 Oklahoma and No. 4 UCLA.
