[Notre Dame](/ncaaf/team/87/notre-dame-fighting-irish) beat No. 1 [Miami](/ncaaf/team/2390/miami-hurricanes) 31-24 on Saturday, holding the highest-scoring offense in FBS to its fewest points this season, per ESPN's scoreboard. Miami entered the game averaging 47.6 points; Notre Dame held it to 24, which is 23.6 below that average.

::

It was the 5th loss by a No. 1 team in 10 weeks. Ohio State fell 23-24 to Texas in Week 2, Texas fell 6-30 to UTSA in Week 3 and 24-27 to Oklahoma in Week 6, and Georgia fell 24-27 to Auburn in Week 7. No. 1 teams are 4-5 in the 9 games they have played.

Notre Dame's defense is a season-long one: it allows 14.6 points a game, the 9th-fewest in FBS. The Irish are 8-1, and their only loss was 24-28 to BYU in Week 7.

Miami is still 8-1 and still leads FBS in scoring at 45.0 points a game. Saturday was the first time all season it scored fewer than 33 — and a 7-point loss to the No. 11 team does not end its season, only its unbeaten one.
