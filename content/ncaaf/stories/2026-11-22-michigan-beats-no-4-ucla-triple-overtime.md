[Michigan Wolverines](/ncaaf/team/130/michigan-wolverines) beat [UCLA Bruins](/ncaaf/team/26/ucla-bruins) 18-16 in triple overtime on Saturday, handing the No. 4 Bruins their first loss after a 10-0 start. Michigan, 6-5, beat the highest-ranked team it has faced this season.

The game was tied 10-10 after regulation, 13-13 after the 1st overtime and 16-16 after the 2nd. In the 3rd overtime, teams alternate 2-point conversions: Michigan converted its try and UCLA did not, ending it 18-16.

Neither offense found much room: UCLA outgained Michigan 296-247, and the teams combined for 4 turnovers — 3 by UCLA, 1 by Michigan. [Savion Hiter](/ncaaf/player/5159046/savion-hiter) ran 26 times for 104 yards, more than any other rusher in the game. [Bryce Underwood](/ncaaf/player/5141741/bryce-underwood) went 11 of 24 for 127 yards and a touchdown.
