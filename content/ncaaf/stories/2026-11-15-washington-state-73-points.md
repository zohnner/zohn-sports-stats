The [Washington State Cougars](/ncaaf/team/265/washington-state-cougars) scored 73 points in Saturday's 73-7 win over the [Colorado State Rams](/ncaaf/team/36/colorado-state-rams), their most in a game since at least 2021 and 25 more than their best this season before Saturday. Washington State is 2-8, and the 73 points are almost triple the 24.4 the Cougars average this season.

The previous high was 70, against Portland State in 2024, and the Cougars' best scoring game this season before Saturday was 48 against Duquesne, an FCS team. Against FBS opponents, their previous best was 30 against Oregon State. The 66-point margin is also the largest since at least 2021, ahead of a 43-point win over Northern Colorado in 2023.

Colorado State's defense offered little resistance, and the 73 points say as much about the Rams as the Cougars. Colorado State is 3-7 and allows 47.6 points a game, the most it had allowed before Saturday was 66, against Texas State, and Washington State still beat that by 7. The Rams scored 7 points, their fewest this season.

The win does not change what Washington State is: a 2-8 team that had lost 8 of its first 9 games. But 73 points from an offense averaging 24.4 is the kind of outlier that shows up once in a season, and it came against an FBS opponent, not an FCS one.
