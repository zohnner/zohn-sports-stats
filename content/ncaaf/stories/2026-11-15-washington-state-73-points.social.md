**Title:** Washington State scored 73 points — its most since at least 2021 — in a 73-7 rout of Colorado State

The 2-8 Cougars nearly tripled their 24.4-points-a-game average on Saturday, beating Colorado State 73-7. It is Washington State's most points in a game since at least 2021 and its largest margin in that span, 66 points. Colorado State allows 47.6 points a game and had never allowed more than 66.
