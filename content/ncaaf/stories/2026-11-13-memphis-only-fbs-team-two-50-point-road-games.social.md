**Title:** Memphis is the only FBS team with 2 50-point road games this season

The Tigers won 59-8 at Charlotte in Week 5 and 52-41 at South Florida in Week 11. 12 other teams have done it once; Memphis is the only team with 2.

The 52 at South Florida came the hard way: Memphis led 21-0 in the 1st quarter, trailed 41-38 in the 4th, then retook the lead on a 76-yard touchdown 30 seconds later. Marcus Stokes accounted for 5 touchdowns for the 3rd straight game, and Jaylin Carter ran for 109 yards and 2 touchdowns to pass 1,000 rushing yards for the season. Memphis is 8-2.
