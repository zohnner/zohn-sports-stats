[Colorado](/ncaaf/team/38/colorado-buffaloes) beat No. 14 [Houston](/ncaaf/team/248/houston-cougars) 42-32 on Friday in Week 11, the Buffaloes' first win over an AP top-15 team since September 2007, per ESPN's rankings and schedule data. The last one was a 27-24 win over No. 3 Oklahoma — 19 years ago.

In the 19 years between, Colorado beat 8 ranked teams — and the best of them was ranked 17th. The Buffaloes lost their first 2 games against top-15 teams this season, 7-29 to No. 12 Texas Tech and 24-27 to No. 11 Utah, before Houston.

Colorado ran for 249 yards on 39 carries, converted 10 of 14 on third down, and scored 42 points. Houston averages 35.1 points a game and had allowed no more than 32 all season; Colorado averages 22.0 and scored 42. The 42 was also Colorado's most against an FBS opponent this season; its previous high was 27 against Kansas State.

The Buffaloes shut Houston out in the 3rd quarter, 14-0, after trailing 3-0 after the 1st quarter. [Richard Young](/ncaaf/player/4871081/richard-young) ran for 99 yards and 2 touchdowns on 21 carries, and [DeKalon Taylor](/ncaaf/player/5096878/dekalon-taylor) scored on a 67-yard run on his only carry. [Isaac Wilson](/ncaaf/player/5079771/isaac-wilson) threw for 217 yards on 17-of-25 passing. For Houston, [Conner Weigman](/ncaaf/player/4685574/conner-weigman) threw for 272 yards and 3 touchdowns and [Makhi Hughes](/ncaaf/player/4816098/makhi-hughes) ran for 106 yards, but Houston outgained Colorado 477-466 and still lost, turning it over 2 times to Colorado's 1.

Colorado is 4-6 and 2-5 in the Big 12; Houston is 7-3 and 4-3. The win is Colorado's first over a ranked team this season, in its 3rd game against one.
