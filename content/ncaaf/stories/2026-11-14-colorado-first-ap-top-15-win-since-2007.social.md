**Title:** Colorado's first AP top-15 win in 19 years: 42-32 over No. 14 Houston

Colorado had not beaten a top-15 team since No. 3 Oklahoma in September 2007. On Friday the Buffaloes beat No. 14 Houston 42-32, running for 249 yards and converting 10 of 14 on third down. In the 19 years between, Colorado's best ranked win was over a No. 17 team.
