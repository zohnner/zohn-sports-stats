**Title:** Washington beat No. 8 Indiana 27-24 in overtime for its first ranked win

Washington trailed 17-14 after 3 quarters, won the fourth quarter 10-7 to force overtime, then kicked the winner while holding Indiana scoreless. Demond Williams Jr. threw for 227 yards and a touchdown and ran for 88 yards and another score, including a 65-yard run. Washington's 24 points are the most Indiana has allowed all season — the previous high was 23.
