The [Arkansas Razorbacks](/ncaaf/team/8/arkansas-razorbacks) held the [South Carolina Gamecocks](/ncaaf/team/2579/south-carolina-gamecocks) to 6 points in Saturday's 34-6 win, the fewest South Carolina has scored this season and 25.8 below the 31.8 the Gamecocks average. South Carolina arrived ranked No. 17 at 6-3; Arkansas was 3-6.

The 6 points are 11 below South Carolina's previous low of 17, against Texas A&M last week, and the Gamecocks had scored at least 27 in 7 of their first 9 games. Arkansas's defense had allowed 43 to Utah and 45 to Georgia earlier this season; on Saturday it held a ranked team to a single score.

Arkansas scored 34 points, tying its season high, and the 28-point margin ties its largest of the season — the same 34-6 score it posted against Tulsa in September. The difference is the opponent: Tulsa is unranked, and South Carolina arrived at 6-3 and ranked No. 17.

A 3-6 team beating a ranked team by 28 points does not make Arkansas a contender — the Razorbacks are 4-6. It does say the Gamecocks' offense, which scored 42 against Oklahoma in late October, ran into a defense that had its best day of the season.
