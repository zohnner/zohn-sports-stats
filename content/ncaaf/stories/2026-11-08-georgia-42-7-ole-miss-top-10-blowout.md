[Georgia](/ncaaf/team/61/georgia-bulldogs) beat No. 10 [Ole Miss](/ncaaf/team/145/ole-miss-rebels) 42-7 on Saturday, the widest margin in a game between top-10 teams this season, per ESPN's scoreboard. The other 6 such games were decided by 8 points or fewer.

::chart bars Margin in top-10 matchups this season
*Georgia 42, Ole Miss 7 | 35
Utah 28, BYU 20 | 8
Ole Miss 32, LSU 24 | 8
BYU 28, Notre Dame 24 | 4
Ohio State 19, Indiana 15 | 4
Georgia 20, Alabama 17 | 3
Texas 24, Ohio State 23 | 1
::

Georgia did it by shutting down an offense that had scored at least 27 points in every game this season. Ole Miss managed 7, its fewest this season and 26.8 below its 33.8-point average. Its previous low was 27, against Texas in Week 8, and it had scored 45 against Auburn a week earlier.

The defense behind the number is a season-long one. Georgia allows 14.0 points a game, the 7th-fewest in FBS. Ole Miss scored 45 against Auburn a week earlier; Georgia held it to 7.

One blowout does not remake a season. Georgia is 8-1, and its only loss was 24-27 at Auburn in Week 7. Ole Miss is 6-3 and still averages 33.8 points a game. What Saturday showed is the gap between them right now: 35 points, in a season where no other top-10 game has been decided by more than 8.
