[Utah Utes](/ncaaf/team/254/utah-utes) are 11-0, the only unbeaten team left in the FBS, after [UCLA Bruins](/ncaaf/team/26/ucla-bruins) lost 18-16 to Michigan in triple overtime on Saturday. Utah and UCLA were the only 2 unbeaten teams entering the weekend, and the Bruins were the other one.

Utah beat [TCU Horned Frogs](/ncaaf/team/2628/tcu-horned-frogs) 38-17 on Saturday. TCU cut a 17-0 halftime deficit to 17-14 with 14 third-quarter points, and Utah answered with 21 points in the fourth quarter to TCU's 3.

[Devon Dampier](/ncaaf/player/5105849/devon-dampier) accounted for 4 touchdowns: 18 of 28 passing for 278 yards and 2 touchdowns, plus 62 rushing yards and 2 more scores on 21 carries. [Larry Simmons](/ncaaf/player/4685704/larry-simmons) caught 3 passes for 114 yards and a touchdown, including an 85-yard score. Utah outgained TCU 421-259, and TCU quarterback Adam Schobel went 15 of 31 for 106 yards and threw 2 interceptions.

Through 11 games, Utah averages 37.9 points and allows 18.5, a gap of 19.4 points a game. The closest call was the 40-39 win over Arizona last Sunday, a 1-point margin; the next-closest was 3 points against Colorado.
