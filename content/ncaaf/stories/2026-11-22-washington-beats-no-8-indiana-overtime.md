[Washington Huskies](/ncaaf/team/264/washington-huskies) beat [Indiana Hoosiers](/ncaaf/team/84/indiana-hoosiers) 27-24 in overtime on Saturday, Washington's first win over a ranked team this season. Indiana, 9-1 entering the game, fell to 9-2.

Indiana led 17-14 after 3 quarters. Washington won the fourth quarter 10-7 to force overtime, then won it with a field goal in overtime while holding Indiana scoreless.

Washington had lost its other 2 games against ranked teams: 25-21 to No. 18 Southern California and 41-24 to No. 20 Iowa. Indiana's only previous loss was 19-15 to Ohio State, and both of Indiana's defeats have been 1-score games.

[Demond Williams Jr.](/ncaaf/player/5079653/demond-williams-jr) went 24 of 41 for 227 yards and a touchdown, and ran 11 times for 88 yards and another score, including a 65-yard run. [Charlie Becker](/ncaaf/player/5146735/charlie-becker) caught 6 passes for 167 yards and a touchdown for Indiana, including a 74-yard catch.

Washington's 24 points are the most Indiana has allowed this season; the previous high was 23, against Northwestern. Washington outgained Indiana 443-392.
