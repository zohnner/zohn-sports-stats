[Oklahoma Sooners](/ncaaf/team/201/oklahoma-sooners) beat the [Ole Miss Rebels](/ncaaf/team/145/ole-miss-rebels) 24-17 on Sunday, and the difference was a play the offense had nothing to do with. [Isaiah Sategna III](/ncaaf/player/5080703/isaiah-sategna-iii) returned a punt 75 yards for a touchdown in the first quarter, and Oklahoma's offense scored 17 points — the same as Ole Miss scored all game.

The Sooners threw for 111 yards — [John Mateer](/ncaaf/player/4915980/john-mateer) went 10 of 19 — and were outgained 306-299. They converted 9 of 16 3rd downs to Ole Miss's 4 of 12, and Mateer ran for a touchdown, but the passing game never got going: Oklahoma's longest completion went for 22 yards.

The win is Oklahoma's 3rd over a ranked team this season, after No. 1 Texas and No. 24 Florida. The Sooners are 6-4, with 3 ranked wins against 2 losses to ranked teams.
