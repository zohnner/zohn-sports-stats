The [Utah Utes](/ncaaf/team/254/utah-utes) stayed unbeaten with a 40-39 win over the [Arizona Wildcats](/ncaaf/team/12/arizona-wildcats) on Saturday, and the defense that carried them there finally cracked. Arizona scored 39 points, the most Utah has allowed this season by 14, and 22.7 more than the 16.3 Utah had allowed on average in its first 9 games.

The previous high was 25, against Cincinnati, and no other opponent had topped 24. Arizona's 39 came in the closest game of Utah's 10-0 season — the Utes' narrowest win before Saturday was by 3, over Colorado — and it took a 70-yard touchdown pass from [Devon Dampier](/ncaaf/player/5105849/devon-dampier) to [Creed Whittemore](/ncaaf/player/4871040/creed-whittemore) with 2:25 left to win it.

Dampier accounted for 4 touchdowns — 2 passing and 2 rushing — with 204 passing yards and 100 rushing yards, 304 yards in all. Whittemore caught 5 passes for 92 yards, including the 70-yard score. The offense bailed out a defense that had its worst day of the season: Utah now allows 18.6 points a game, up from 16.3 entering Saturday.

Utah is 10-0, one of 2 unbeaten teams left in the FBS alongside UCLA, also 10-0. A 1-point win over a 7-3 Arizona team is not the way a No. 2 team wants to survive November, but the record is the record: 10-0, with the defense's worst day already behind it.
