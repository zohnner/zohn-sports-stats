**Title:** Oregon has allowed 7 points in back-to-back games

Oregon beat Michigan State 45-7 on Friday, holding the Spartans to 200 total yards and forcing 2 turnovers. The Ducks had allowed 22.1 points a game through their first 9 games, and have now allowed 7 in each of their last 2 — the only times all season they have held an FBS opponent to single digits. Brandon Smith ran for 121 yards and 2 touchdowns on 14 carries.
