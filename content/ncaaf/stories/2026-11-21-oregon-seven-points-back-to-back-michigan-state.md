The [Oregon Ducks](/ncaaf/team/2483/oregon-ducks) beat [Michigan State](/ncaaf/team/127/michigan-state-spartans) 45-7 on Friday, and the score flattered the Spartans' offense: Oregon held Michigan State to 200 total yards and forced 2 turnovers.

Oregon had allowed 22.1 points a game through its first 9 games. It has allowed 7 in each of the last 2 games — the only times all season it has held an FBS opponent to single digits. The one shutout on Oregon's schedule came against Portland State, an FCS team, in September.

[Brandon Smith](/ncaaf/player/5198506/brandon-smith) ran for 121 yards and 2 touchdowns on 14 carries, and Oregon's 45 points were its most against an FBS opponent this season. The previous high was 41, against USC and Ohio State.

Oregon outgained Michigan State 422-200 and did not turn the ball over, while the Spartans lost 2. Dylan Raiola went 21 of 34 for 234 yards and a touchdown, and Smith's longest run went for 48 yards.

Oregon is 8-3 and has won 4 straight. The Ducks were ranked No. 13 in the AP poll entering the game.
