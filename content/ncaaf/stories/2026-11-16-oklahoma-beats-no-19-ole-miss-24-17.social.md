**Title:** Oklahoma beat No. 19 Ole Miss on a 75-yard punt return

The Sooners beat No. 19 Ole Miss 24-17 on Sunday, and the difference was a play the offense had nothing to do with: Isaiah Sategna III returned a punt 75 yards for a touchdown in the first quarter. Oklahoma threw for 111 yards and was outgained 306-299, and its offense scored 17 points — the same as Ole Miss scored all game.
