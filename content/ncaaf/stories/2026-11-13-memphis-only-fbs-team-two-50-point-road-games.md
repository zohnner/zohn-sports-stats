[Memphis](/ncaaf/team/235/memphis-tigers) is the only team in the Bowl Subdivision with 50 or more points in 2 road games this season, per ESPN's scoreboards. The Tigers won 59-8 at Charlotte in Week 5 and 52-41 at [South Florida](/ncaaf/team/58/south-florida-bulls) in Week 11. 12 other teams have done it once; Memphis is the only team with 2.

::chart bars 50-point road games this season
*Memphis | 2
12 teams | 1
::

The 52 at South Florida came the hard way. Memphis led 21-0 in the 1st quarter, then watched South Florida climb all the way back to take a 41-38 lead in the 4th quarter. Memphis answered in 30 seconds: Marcus Stokes hit Jemyri Davis for a 76-yard touchdown to retake the lead 45-41, and Jaylin Carter sealed it with 44 seconds left.

Stokes accounted for 5 touchdowns for the 3rd straight game: 3 through the air and 2 on the ground, with 313 passing yards and 73 rushing yards. It was his 4th straight 300-yard passing game, and his 47-yard touchdown run in the 1st quarter was the longest by a Memphis quarterback in 30 seasons.

Carter ran for 109 yards and 2 touchdowns on 21 carries, and his 1,038 rushing yards this season make him the 12th FBS rusher to reach 1,000 yards. He is the 10th Memphis running back to reach 1,000 yards in a season, and the first since Mario Anderson ran for 1,362 yards in the 2024 season.

Memphis gained 581 yards, its 6th game over 500 yards this season, and sacked South Florida 7 times, a season high. Memphis is 8-2 and 5-1 in the American Conference; South Florida is 6-4 and 2-4.

The only-team claim holds through Week 11. The season is not over, and another team can join Memphis with a 50-point road game in the weeks ahead.
