The [Detroit Pistons](/nba/team/det) held the [Orlando Magic](/nba/team/orl) to 86 points in a 104-86 win at the Kia Center on Tuesday, the fewest Detroit has allowed in a game this season and the fewest Orlando has scored in one. Detroit's previous low allowed was 89 against Charlotte, and Orlando's previous low was 93 against Minnesota.

Orlando shot 34 of 83 from the field (41%) and 12 of 36 from 3-point range, and committed 20 turnovers. Detroit scored 16 points off those turnovers and 19 fast-break points, and led by as many as 18.

Detroit did it without [Cade Cunningham](/nba/player/4432166/cade-cunningham), who did not play. The Pistons are 2-0 in games he has missed this season, also beating New Orleans 122-109 on Nov 18. [Daniss Jenkins](/nba/player/5107199/daniss-jenkins) started at point guard and had 15 points and 10 assists, and [Isaiah Joe](/nba/player/4395702/isaiah-joe) scored 21 points on 5-of-11 shooting from 3-point range.

Detroit allows 107.2 points a game, the 5th-fewest in the NBA, against a league average of 113.5. The Pistons are 11-6 and 8-3 on the road. Orlando fell to 7-10 and is 1-6 at home.
