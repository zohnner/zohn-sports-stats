The [San Antonio Spurs](/nba/standings) lost their first 2 games of the season, 116-104 to Oklahoma City and 114-110 to Houston. They have won 7 straight since, the longest win streak in the NBA, and they are 7-2.

## 7 wins, none of them close except one

The streak has not been close: the 7 wins came by 97 points combined, 13.9 a game, with 4 at home and 3 on the road. The only 1-point game of the run was the Dallas win built on Wembanyama's 52-point night; the other 6 were decided by 6 or more.

## Wembanyama's 52-point night

[Victor Wembanyama](/nba/player/5104157/victor-wembanyama) scored 52 points against Dallas on Wednesday, with 24 rebounds and 8 blocks, in a 112-111 win. The 52 is a career high — his previous best was 50, against Washington in the 2024-25 season. He followed it with 30 points and 14 rebounds at Sacramento on Friday, in a 124-109 win, and he is averaging 29.1 points a game, 4th in the NBA.

## The schedule question

The 7 wins came against opponents now a combined 21-43, with Dallas, Sacramento and Utah each faced 2 times. But the wins are not just the schedule. Those opponents average 113.0 points in their other games, and 106.9 against San Antonio, a gap of 6.1 points a game. San Antonio scores 120.7 a game in the 7 wins. The streak goes to Portland on Sunday.
