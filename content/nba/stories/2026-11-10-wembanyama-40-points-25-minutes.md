[Victor Wembanyama](/nba/player/5104157/victor-wembanyama) scored 40 points in 25 minutes against Phoenix on Monday night, with 11 rebounds and 9 blocks, in San Antonio's 122-96 win. He shot 15-of-19 from the field and 8-of-10 from the line, for 1.6 points per minute played.

## His 2nd 40-point game in 5 days

Wembanyama scored 52 against Dallas 5 days earlier, with 24 rebounds and 8 blocks. The 40 against Phoenix is his 2nd 40-point game in 5 days, and it came in barely half a game: 25 of the 48 minutes.

The 9 blocks are double his league-leading average of 4.5 a game. He averages 30.1 points a game, 3rd in the NBA behind Luka Doncic and Stephen Curry.

## The Spurs have won 9 straight

The win is San Antonio's 9th straight, taking the Spurs to 9-2 with the league's best point differential, plus-11.7 a game. Wembanyama is the driver: he has scored 40 or more in 2 of the 9 wins.

25 minutes is a small slice of a season, and one night against Phoenix does not settle anything. The numbers in it stand on their own: 40 points, 11 rebounds and 9 blocks, at 1.6 points a minute, from a player averaging 30.1 points a game.
