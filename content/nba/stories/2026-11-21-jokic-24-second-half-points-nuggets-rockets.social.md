**Title:** Jokic's 24-point second half followed a 2-point first half

Nikola Jokic scored 2 points in the first half against Houston on Friday, then 24 in the second half — 18 in the third quarter alone — as Denver beat the Rockets 133-124. Jokic finished with 26 points and 11 assists, and Denver pulled away with a 34-22 fourth quarter. Alperen Sengun had 28 points, 11 rebounds and 10 assists for Houston.
