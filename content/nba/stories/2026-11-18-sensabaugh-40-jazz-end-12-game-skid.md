[Brice Sensabaugh](/nba/player/5105839/brice-sensabaugh) scored a season-high 40 points off the bench, including 25 in the 4th quarter, as the [Utah Jazz](/nba/team/uta) beat the [LA Clippers](/nba/team/lac) 132-107 on Tuesday — Utah's first win in 25 days, ending a 12-game losing streak.

Sensabaugh shot 16 of 21 from the field and 8 of 11 from 3-point range, a career high in made 3s, with 11 rebounds and 5 assists. It was his 3rd career 40-point game and his first 40-point double-double, 3 points short of his career high. He made 10 of 11 shots in the 4th quarter.

Utah led 88-86 after 3 quarters, then outscored the Clippers 44-21 in the 4th, including a 16-0 run. [Keyonte George](/nba/player/4433627/keyonte-george) added 30 points and 11 assists. Utah made a season-high 20 threes and shot 58 percent from the field, and its 132 points were a season high.

Utah is 2-13, and the streak covered every game since a 120-119 win over New Orleans on Oct 23. The Clippers fell to 4-9.
