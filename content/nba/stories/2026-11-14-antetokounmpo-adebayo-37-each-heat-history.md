[Giannis Antetokounmpo](/nba/player/3032977/giannis-antetokounmpo) and [Bam Adebayo](/nba/player/4066261/bam-adebayo) each scored 37 points in the [Miami Heat](/nba/team/mia)' 133-121 win over the [Cleveland Cavaliers](/nba/team/cle) on Friday, becoming the 2nd pair of Heat teammates to each score at least 35 points in the same game, and the first since LeBron James and Dwyane Wade did it in 2013. James and Wade are the other pair: Wade scored 36 and James 35 in a loss at Chicago on March 14, 2012, and James scored 40 and Wade 39 in a double-overtime win over Sacramento on Feb. 26, 2013.

The pair combined for 74 points on 28-of-39 shooting — Antetokounmpo 15 of 20 from the field, Adebayo 13 of 19 — and Adebayo added 14 rebounds with 0 turnovers in 38 minutes. Antetokounmpo played 31 minutes and grabbed 9 rebounds.

[Davion Mitchell](/nba/player/4278053/davion-mitchell) added 22 points and 17 assists, and [Ryan Conwell](/nba/player/5107157/ryan-conwell) scored 20, giving Miami 4 players with at least 20 points. The 133 points were Miami's 3rd-highest total of the season, behind the 145 against Indiana and the 137 against Detroit on Saturday. Cleveland, which got 25 points from Donovan Mitchell, is 7-7 after beating Dallas on Saturday.

Miami is 7-6 after beating Detroit 137-117 on Saturday, a 4th straight win after an 0-4 start. One night does not remake a 7-6 team — but they are the first Heat duo to each score 35-plus since LeBron and Wade in 2013.
