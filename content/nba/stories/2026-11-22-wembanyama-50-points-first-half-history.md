[Victor Wembanyama](/nba/player/5104157/victor-wembanyama) scored 32 points in the first half of [San Antonio's](/nba/team/sa) 114-91 win over [New Orleans](/nba/team/no) on Saturday, in nearly 18 first-half minutes, and finished with 50 points, 19 rebounds, 5 blocks and 4 steals in 32 minutes.

He became the first player in the play-by-play era to record at least 30 points, 10 rebounds, 3 blocks and 3 steals in a half, per ESPN. His first-half line also included 3 blocks and 3 steals, both counted from the play-by-play.

It was his 3rd career 50-point game, tying [LeBron James](/nba/player/1966/lebron-james) and [Devin Booker](/nba/player/3136193/devin-booker) for 2nd-most by a player 22 or younger. He also joined David Robinson as the only players with multiple 50-point, 5-block games since blocks were first tracked.

Wembanyama shot 20 of 29 from the field and 5 of 11 from 3. San Antonio led by 29 at one point and shot 19 of 56 from 3 as a team; New Orleans shot 37% from the field.
