[Jalen Johnson](/nba/player/4701230/jalen-johnson) posted his 2nd triple-double in 5 days — 16 points, 12 rebounds and 11 assists — as the [Atlanta Hawks](/nba/team/atl) beat the [Oklahoma City Thunder](/nba/team/okc) 117-112 on Wednesday, handing Oklahoma City its first home loss of the season.

The Thunder had been 5-0 at home, winning those games by 12, 7, 11, 8 and 39 points. [Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](/nba/player/4278073/shai-gilgeous-alexander) scored 34 points for Oklahoma City on 14-of-29 shooting, but Atlanta held on for the win.

Johnson's first triple-double of the week came Friday at Chicago: 27 points, 11 rebounds and 10 assists in a 125-117 overtime loss. 2 triple-doubles in 5 days, and the 2nd one came against a team that had not lost at home.

[Onyeka Okongwu](/nba/player/4431680/onyeka-okongwu) added 28 points for Atlanta, making 5 of 13 from 3-point range. The Hawks are 9-6, their 2nd straight win after the overtime loss in Chicago.
