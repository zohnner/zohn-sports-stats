[Jaden McDaniels](/nba/player/4431671/jaden-mcdaniels) scored a career-high 34 points as the [Minnesota Timberwolves](/nba/team/min) beat the [Brooklyn Nets](/nba/team/bkn) 142-116 on Monday, the most points Minnesota has scored this season.

McDaniels made 12 of 16 shots and added 10 rebounds and 6 assists. His previous career high was 30, and his best in 18 games this season had been 25.

Minnesota shot 51 of 88 from the field, 58%, and made 17 of 39 from 3-point range. The 142 points topped its previous high of 133, against Dallas, by 9.

Ayo Dosunmu added 23 points for Minnesota. Brooklyn shot 53% and got 29 points from Julius Randle, and still lost by 26.

Minnesota is 13-5. Brooklyn fell to 3-13.
