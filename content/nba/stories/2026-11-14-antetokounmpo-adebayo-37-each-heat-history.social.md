**Title:** Giannis and Bam each scored 37 — the 2nd Heat duo to each score 35-plus

Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo each scored 37 points in Miami's 133-121 win over Cleveland, combining for 74 points on 28-of-39 shooting. They are the 2nd pair of Heat teammates to each score at least 35 in the same game — the first since LeBron James and Dwyane Wade in 2013. Davion Mitchell added 22 points and 17 assists as Miami won its 3rd straight after an 0-4 start.
