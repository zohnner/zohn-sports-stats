[Luka Doncic](/nba/player/3945274/luka-doncic) and the Los Angeles Lakers score 121.9 points a game, the most in the NBA, per ESPN's standings. Portland is next at 119.3, a gap of 2.6 points. The Lakers have scored 1,341 points in 11 games, 8.6 above the league average of 113.3.

## Doncic drives it, and the scoring does not stop with him

Doncic averages 40.1 points a game, 8.9 clear of [Stephen Curry](/nba/player/3975/stephen-curry) at 31.2, and his 361 points are 26.9% of the Lakers' total. On Sunday, with Doncic not playing, Los Angeles still scored 121 in a 121-116 win at Detroit, with [Austin Reaves](/nba/player/4066457/austin-reaves) scoring 43 points on 14-of-27 shooting.

The lead is real but young. Portland has played 9 games to Los Angeles's 11, so the per-game comparison is the fair one, and 11 games is 11 games. The Lakers are also 9-2, tied with Philadelphia for the league's best record, so the scoring lead sits on top of winning, not instead of it.
