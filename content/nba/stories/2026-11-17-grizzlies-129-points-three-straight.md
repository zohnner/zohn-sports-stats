The [Memphis Grizzlies](/nba/team/mem) have scored 129 or more in 3 straight games — 142 against New Orleans, 129 against Brooklyn and 136 at Washington — the longest such run of their season. The 3-game average of 135.7 points is 14.0 more than the 121.7 they average on the season, the 2nd-highest scoring average in the NBA behind the Lakers' 122.8. Their previous longest run of 129 or more was 2 games, in early November.

Memphis scored | Opponents usually allow
New Orleans | 142 | 121.5
Brooklyn | 129 | 116.9
Washington | 136 | 118.0
::

The gap holds after adjusting for the opponents. Memphis beat what each of those 3 teams allows in its other games by double digits every time, 16.9 points a game on average — 20.5 more than New Orleans allows, 12.1 more than Brooklyn and 18.0 more than Washington. The scoring is not only the schedule.

[Cameron Boozer](/nba/player/5041935/cameron-boozer) led Memphis with 29 points, 7 rebounds and 7 assists in Monday's 136-120 win at Washington, and [Zach Edey](/nba/player/4600663/zach-edey) added 23 points and 10 rebounds. Memphis is 10-4, and the run has come with the wins: all 3 games were victories, by 31, 19 and 16 points.
