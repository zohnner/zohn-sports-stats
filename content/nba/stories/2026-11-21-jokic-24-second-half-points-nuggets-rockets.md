[Nikola Jokic](/nba/player/3112335/nikola-jokic) scored 2 points in the first half against Houston on Friday. He scored 24 in the second half — 18 in the third quarter alone — and the [Denver Nuggets](/nba/team/den) beat the [Houston Rockets](/nba/team/hou) 133-124.

Jokic finished with 26 points and 11 assists. He made 8 shots in the third quarter, and Denver pulled away with a 34-22 fourth quarter after Houston had outscored the Nuggets 42-37 in the third quarter. Denver led 62-60 at halftime despite Jokic's 2 points.

[Alperen Sengun](/nba/player/4871144/alperen-sengun) had 28 points, 11 rebounds and 10 assists for Houston, a triple-double in a losing effort. Houston fell to 12-5; Denver improved to 9-5.

The win avenged a 110-100 loss to the same Rockets 10 days earlier, and Denver's 133 points were its 2nd-highest total of the season, behind only a 141-point night against New Orleans in October.
