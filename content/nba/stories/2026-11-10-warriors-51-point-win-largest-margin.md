The [Golden State Warriors](/nba/standings) beat New Orleans 138-87 on Monday night, a 51-point margin that is the largest in the NBA this season, per ESPN's scoreboard. The previous largest was 47, when Atlanta beat Charlotte 128-81 on Oct 23.

## Nobody had to play more than 27 minutes

This was depth, not a star night. Stephen Curry scored 25 points in 21 minutes, on 6-of-10 shooting with 4 3-pointers and a 9-of-9 line at the free-throw stripe. Brandin Podziemski scored 25 in 26 minutes, and Yaxel Lendeborg scored 23 in 27 minutes, on 8-of-10 shooting with 4 3-pointers. De'Anthony Melton added 16 in 26 minutes. No Warrior played more than 27 minutes.

New Orleans had no answer at either end. No Pelican scored more than 12 points, Dejounte Murray's total, and the 87 points are 26.4 below the league average of 113.4 a game.

## The streak is 8, but the margin is the story

The win is Golden State's 8th straight, taking the Warriors to 8-3. They score 120.4 points a game, 2nd in the NBA behind the Lakers at 122.1.

The margin is the finding, not a claim about where Golden State ranks: the previous largest margin of the season also came against a struggling team, Atlanta's 47-point win over Charlotte, now 1-8. The 51 stands on its own as the season's biggest blowout.
