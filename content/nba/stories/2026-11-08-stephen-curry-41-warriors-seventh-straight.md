[Stephen Curry](/nba/player/3975/stephen-curry) scored 41 points on 13-of-19 shooting as the [Golden State Warriors](/nba/standings) beat Orlando 126-119 on Saturday, per ESPN's box score. It was his 2nd 40-point game in 8 games this season, and it moved him past [Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](/nba/player/4278073/shai-gilgeous-alexander) into 2nd in NBA scoring.

Curry now averages 31.2 points a game, behind only [Luka Doncic](/nba/player/3945274/luka-doncic) at 40.1 and 1.3 clear of Gilgeous-Alexander at 29.9. He has 250 points in 8 games. The 41 came on 68.4% shooting from the field, 5 of 10 from 3-point range. His other 40-point game, 44 against Minnesota, took 27 shots on 40.7% shooting.

The win was Golden State's 7th straight after an 0-3 start. The Warriors lost their first 3 games by 22, 3 and 9 points, then won 7 in a row, 5 of them at home. Curry has played 8 of their 10 games.

8 games is a small sample, and the scoring race will move — Doncic leads Curry by 8.9 points a game. But Curry's place in it is now clear: 2nd in the NBA, 1.3 clear of Gilgeous-Alexander, on the back of the most efficient 40-point night of his season.
