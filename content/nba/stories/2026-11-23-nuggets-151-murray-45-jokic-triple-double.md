[Denver Nuggets](/nba/team/den) beat the [Chicago Bulls](/nba/team/chi) 151-145 in overtime on Sunday, scoring the most points by any NBA team this season. The previous high was 147, by Houston a week earlier, and no team scored more than 137 since.

[Jamal Murray](/nba/player/3936299/jamal-murray) scored 45 points on 13-of-22 shooting, including 6 of 11 from 3-point range, and [Nikola Jokic](/nba/player/3112335/nikola-jokic) had 37 points, 18 rebounds and 11 assists. Together they scored 82 of Denver's 151 points. Denver shot 53% from the field, 17 of 35 from 3-point range, and 26 of 30 at the line.

Chicago kept pace: [Josh Giddey](/nba/player/4871145/josh-giddey) had 23 points, 14 rebounds and 22 assists, and the Bulls shot 25 of 55 from 3-point range. The game was tied 131-131 after regulation; Denver outscored Chicago 20-14 in overtime. The 2 teams combined for 296 points.

Denver is 10-5; Chicago fell to 4-12.
