[Karl-Anthony Towns](/nba/player/3136195/karl-anthony-towns) has a double-double in every game the [New York Knicks](/nba/standings) have played this season — 11 in 11 — the most in the NBA, per ESPN's leaders. He averages 13.5 rebounds a game, also the league's best, on 20.9 points a game.

The 11th came Wednesday against Cleveland: 21 points, 11 rebounds and 8 assists in a 115-107 loss that ended New York's 4-game winning streak. Towns was 2 assists short of a triple-double, and the Knicks are now 6-5.

The streak ties Willis Reed's franchise record of 11 straight double-doubles to open a season, set in the 1970-71 season. Only Walt Bellamy, with 13 in the 1965-66 season, has opened a Knicks season with a longer run.

## The only perfect record in the league

Towns is the only player in the NBA with a double-double in every game he has played. Giannis Antetokounmpo has 9 in 11 games, Nikola Jokic 8 in 10, Victor Wembanyama 8 in 12, and Domantas Sabonis 7 in 10. The rest of the top 5 have all missed at least 2 times; Towns has not missed once.

11 games is early, and a double-double is the counting line a starting center reaches most easily: Towns starts every game and plays 31.4 minutes a night. The run stands apart because of the company, not the counting — the last Knicks player to open a season this way was Reed, 56 years ago.

The Knicks visit Indiana on Friday.
