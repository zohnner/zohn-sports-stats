[Boston Celtics](/nba/team/bos/boston-celtics) beat the [Dallas Mavericks](/nba/team/dal) 131-94 on Sunday, a 37-point win that moved Boston past San Antonio into the league lead in point differential: plus-13.1 a game, ahead of the Spurs' plus-12.9 and Minnesota's plus-11.5. San Antonio led at plus-13.2 entering Sunday; Boston was 3rd at plus-11.2.

The gap is the defense. Boston allows 102.4 points a game, the fewest in the NBA — New York is next at 104.0 — and the Celtics have held 10 of 13 opponents under 110. Jayson Tatum scored 32 points on 11-of-18 shooting in 30 minutes, and Derrick White added 16.

Boston is 11-2 with 6 straight wins, and the 131 was its 2nd-highest total of the season, behind the 134 it scored against Chicago.
