The [New York Knicks](/nba/standings) allow 102.3 points a game, the fewest in the NBA through 10 games. Boston is next at 104.0, and Detroit at 104.6. The Knicks are 6-4, on a 4-game win streak, after starting 0-3.

The turnaround is the defense. New York allowed 106, 107 and 111 in its first 3 games, 108.0 a game, and lost all 3. Since then it has won 6 of 7, allowing 99.9 a game. Only the 127 against Chicago breaks the pattern, and the Knicks still won that game, 133-127.

## 5 of 7 under 100

5 of the last 7 opponents scored under 100, and the 85 against Milwaukee is the fewest New York has allowed this season; the 90 against Brooklyn is the next-lowest. New York scores 110.6 a game, for a differential of plus-8.3, behind Boston's plus-11.7 in the East.

The sample is 10 games, and 4 of the 6 wins came against teams with losing records. But the shape of the turnaround is what the standings do not show: the Knicks went from allowing 108.0 a game in 3 losses to 99.9 in 6 wins and 1 loss.
