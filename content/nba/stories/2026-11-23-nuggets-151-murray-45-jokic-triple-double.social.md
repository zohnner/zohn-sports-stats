**Title:** Denver scored 151, the NBA's most this season, behind Murray's 45

Jamal Murray scored 45 points on 13-of-22 shooting and Nikola Jokic had 37 points, 18 rebounds and 11 assists as Denver beat Chicago 151-145 in overtime. The 151 points are the most by any NBA team this season — the previous high was Houston's 147 — and the 2 teams combined for 296 points. Josh Giddey had 23 points, 14 rebounds and 22 assists in the loss.
