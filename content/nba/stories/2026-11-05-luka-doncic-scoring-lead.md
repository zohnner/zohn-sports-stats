[Luka Doncic](/nba/player/3945274/luka-doncic) is averaging 38.5 points per game through 8 games, the most in the NBA, per ESPN's leaders. [Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](/nba/player/4278073/shai-gilgeous-alexander) is next at 30.1, so Doncic leads the scoring race by 8.4 points a game.

## 5 40-point games in 8 nights

His 8 games, in order: 28, 23, 52, 41, 37, 45, 42, 40. After 28 and 23 to open the season, he has scored at least 37 in each game since, 6 straight games, and 5 of the 8 have reached 40. The 52 came at Utah, the 45 against Toronto, the 42 against Miami, and the 40 at Sacramento on Wednesday night.

## The shot behind the scoring

Doncic also leads the league in 3-pointers made per game, at 5.9; [Stephen Curry](/nba/player/3975/stephen-curry) and [Anthony Edwards](/nba/player/4594268/anthony-edwards) are next at 4.5. The scoring is not a hot week from mid-range: it is built on volume from deep, and it has held against 7 different opponents in those 8 games.

## What the scoring has not done

The [Los Angeles Lakers](/nba/standings) are 7-2, and 6-2 in the 8 games Doncic has played; they won the 1 game he missed, 103-87 at the Clippers. His 308 points are 28.1% of the Lakers' 1,096 points this season. The scoring has a cost on the other side of the ledger: Doncic turns it over 5.1 times a game, and only Darius Garland, at 5.5, turns it over more.

Through 8 games, the scoring lead is not a rounding error: it is wider than the spread across the next 5 scorers. The average will move, as it always does this early, but the gap is the story, and it grew with every 40-point night.
