**Title:** Karl-Anthony Towns has a double-double in all 11 Knicks games this season, tying Willis Reed's franchise record

Towns is 11-for-11 to open the season: 21 points, 11 rebounds and 8 assists in Wednesday's 115-107 loss to Cleveland, which ended the Knicks' 4-game winning streak. He averages 13.5 rebounds a game, the league's best.

The streak ties Willis Reed's 11 from the 1970-71 season; only Walt Bellamy's 13 from the 1965-66 season is longer in Knicks history. Towns is the only player in the league with a double-double in every game he has played — Giannis Antetokounmpo has 9 in 11 games, Nikola Jokic 8 in 10.

The Knicks are 6-5 and visit Indiana on Friday.
