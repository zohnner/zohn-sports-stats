**Title:** The Warriors beat New Orleans by 51, the NBA's largest margin this season — and nobody on Golden State played more than 27 minutes

Golden State won 138-87 on Monday night. The previous largest margin this season was 47, Atlanta's win over Charlotte on Oct 23.

This was depth, not a star night: Stephen Curry scored 25 points in 21 minutes, Brandin Podziemski scored 25 in 26, and Yaxel Lendeborg scored 23 in 27. No Pelican scored more than 12 points.

The win is Golden State's 8th straight, taking the Warriors to 8-3. They score 120.4 points a game, 2nd in the NBA behind the Lakers at 122.1.
