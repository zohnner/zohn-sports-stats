[Quentin Grimes](/nba/player/4397014/quentin-grimes) scored a career-high 47 points for the [Los Angeles Lakers](/nba/team/lal), including 21 in the 4th quarter, as Los Angeles rallied from 18 points down to beat the [Charlotte Hornets](/nba/team/cha) 118-114 in overtime on Tuesday, its 9th straight win.

Grimes made 10 of 14 from 3-point range, joining Kobe Bryant as the only Lakers with 10 or more 3s in a game. Bryant made 12 against Seattle, the only other time a Laker reached 10. Grimes scored 24 points in the first 3 quarters, then 21 in the 4th; his 10th 3 tied the game at 111 and forced overtime.

[Luka Doncic](/nba/player/3945274/luka-doncic) scored 36 points with 7 assists; the pair combined for 83 points and 15 threes. Los Angeles trailed 94-81 after 3 quarters and did not lead again until overtime. Grimes is the 3rd Laker to score 40 or more in the season's first 15 games, after Doncic and [Austin Reaves](/nba/player/4066457/austin-reaves) — only the 4th team in league history to have 3 different 40-point scorers that early, per ESPN Research.

The Lakers are 13-2, the best record in the NBA, and 9-0 in November. They have won 9 straight since a loss at Golden State, and the 9 wins include 5 on the road.
