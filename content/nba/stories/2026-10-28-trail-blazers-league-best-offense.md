The [Portland Trail Blazers](/nba/standings) score 129.3 points per game, the most of the NBA's 30 teams, per ESPN's standings. Portland is 3-0 after beating Los Angeles 136-129. Los Angeles, next at 128.3 points per game, is the only other team above 128.

- Phoenix: won 124-83
- Sacramento: won 128-120

## Where the points come from

The win at Los Angeles came with balance: 4 Portland players scored 21 or more, with Deni Avdija at 23, Ja Morant at 23, Jrue Holiday at 22, and Damian Lillard at 21, against a Lakers team that got 41 from Luka Doncic and 26 from Austin Reaves, 67 combined. Portland's 136 points were the most by any team in the 13 games across the last 2 nights. The margins have shrunk with each game: 41, then 8, then 7.

## Not just a soft schedule

The skeptic's case is the schedule: Phoenix and Sacramento have losing records. But Los Angeles was 3-0 when Portland visited, and Portland still scored 136.

## The caveat

The caveat: 3 games is 3 games, and the lead over Los Angeles is narrow. What can be said: through 3 games, no team in the NBA scores more than Portland.
