[Scottie Barnes](/nba/player/4433134/scottie-barnes) scored 29 points on 14-of-21 shooting, and the [Toronto Raptors](/nba/team/tor) beat the [Detroit Pistons](/nba/team/det) 105-102 on Friday to end a 6-game losing streak. Toronto led at every quarter break and held on while Detroit closed with a 30-point fourth quarter.

The skid was Toronto's longest of the season. The Raptors had not won since beating Milwaukee, and 5 of the 6 losses came by 11 points or fewer — the only wider margin was 16 against Memphis.

Barnes added 5 rebounds, 5 assists and 1 steal in 37 minutes. He did it without a 3-pointer: Barnes was 0 of 0 from deep and scored all 29 points inside the arc. Allen Graves had 20 points and 10 rebounds for Toronto, and Immanuel Quickley had 18 points and 10 assists. Cade Cunningham led Detroit with 23 points and 9 assists.

Detroit is 3-3 at home and 7-2 on the road, so the loss fits a pattern: the Pistons have been better away from Detroit than in it. Toronto, now 6-10, has won 4 of its 8 road games.
