The [Orlando Magic](/nba/team/orl) have won 4 straight games after a 2-7 start, and the latest was their best: a 115-105 win at Boston on Monday that handed the [Boston Celtics](/nba/team/bos) their first home loss of the season. Boston had won its first 6 home games. Orlando is 6-7.

The streak runs 107-100 at Sacramento, 123-108 at Utah, 107-99 over Milwaukee and 115-105 at Boston. It is Orlando's first win streak of the season — before it, the Magic had not won 2 in a row.

3 of the 4 wins came against Sacramento, Utah and Milwaukee, who are a combined 3-38 — the 3 worst records in the league. Sacramento is 1-12, Utah 1-13 and Milwaukee 1-13. The Boston win is the one that says something: Boston is 11-3 with the league's 2nd-best point differential at plus-11.5 a game.

[Desmond Bane](/nba/player/4066320/desmond-bane) scored 24 points and [Franz Wagner](/nba/player/4566434/franz-wagner) had 21 points, 8 rebounds and 9 assists in the Boston win; [Jayson Tatum](/nba/player/4065648/jayson-tatum) had 24 points and 12 rebounds for Boston. The streak's next test is whether the Magic can do it against a team that is not at the bottom — Boston was the first such test, and they passed it.
