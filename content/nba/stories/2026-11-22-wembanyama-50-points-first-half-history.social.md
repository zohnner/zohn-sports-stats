**Title:** Wembanyama scored 50 with 5 blocks after a 32-point first half — a play-by-play-era first

Victor Wembanyama had 32 points in the first half against New Orleans — in nearly 18 minutes — and finished with 50 points, 19 rebounds, 5 blocks and 4 steals in a 114-91 Spurs win. He is the first player in the play-by-play era with at least 30 points, 10 rebounds, 3 blocks and 3 steals in a half. It was his 3rd career 50-point game, tying LeBron James and Devin Booker for 2nd-most by a player 22 or younger.
