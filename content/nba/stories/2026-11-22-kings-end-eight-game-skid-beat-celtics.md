[Sacramento Kings](/nba/team/sac) beat the [Boston Celtics](/nba/team/bos) 97-96 on Saturday, ending an 8-game losing streak. [Keegan Murray](/nba/player/4594327/keegan-murray) tipped in the winner with 5.0 seconds left, after Boston had taken a 96-95 lead on a Payton Pritchard jumper with 11.8 seconds left.

Sacramento had not won since beating Dallas 115-100. Of the 8 losses since then, 6 came by 15 points or fewer, and the closest was a 2-point loss to the Lakers. The lead changed hands 15 times against Boston.

[Darius Acuff Jr.](/nba/player/5142620/darius-acuff-jr) led Sacramento with 21 points and 8 assists, [De'Andre Hunter](/nba/player/4065732/deandre-hunter) added 20, and [Domantas Sabonis](/nba/player/3155942/domantas-sabonis) had 15 points and 17 rebounds. Murray finished with 15 points and 12 rebounds.

Boston shot 34% from the field and 13 of 58 from 3-point range, 22%. Sacramento won despite 20 turnovers to Boston's 11, making 26 of 32 free throws to Boston's 15 of 25.
