[Golden State](/nba/team/gs) lost 126-98 at [Memphis](/nba/team/mem) on Thursday, its 5th straight loss. All 5 have come on the road, and by an average of 23.2 points. The Warriors were 8-3 when the skid began; they are 8-8 now.

The 5 losses: 115-108 at [Houston](/nba/team/hou), 123-85 at [San Antonio](/nba/team/sa), 115-94 at [New Orleans](/nba/team/no), 116-94 at [Dallas](/nba/team/dal), and 126-98 at Memphis.

Golden State has scored under 100 points in 4 of the 5 defeats — 85 at San Antonio, 94 at New Orleans, 94 at Dallas, 98 at Memphis — and its only triple-digit night in the skid was 108 at Houston.

Against Memphis the offense stalled completely: 5 of 29 from 3-point range, 17%, with 22 turnovers, while Memphis shot 52% and had 34 assists. [Charles Bassey](/nba/player/4397886/charles-bassey) led Golden State with 16 points.

Golden State's last 5-game losing streak ended in March 2026. Memphis, meanwhile, has won 5 straight and is 12-4.
