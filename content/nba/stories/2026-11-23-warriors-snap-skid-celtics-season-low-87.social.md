**Title:** The Warriors ended a 5-game skid by holding Boston to a season-low 87 points

Golden State beat Boston 88-87, ending a 5-game losing streak in which all 5 losses came on the road. Boston's 87 points are its fewest in 17 games this season — the previous low was 93 — and the Celtics shot 39% from the field and scored 12 points in the 4th quarter. Brandin Podziemski led Golden State with 18 points; Jayson Tatum had 27 points and 10 rebounds for Boston.
