The [Milwaukee Bucks](/nba/standings) are 1-10, their worst start through 11 games in 23 seasons. No Milwaukee team in the 23 seasons since 2004 has started worse: the previous low was 2-9, by the team that finished 15-67, and last season's team started 7-4. The only win this season came against Indiana, 110-102.

The 7 losses have come by 20.3 points a game. Only one of the 7 came by fewer than 10 points, and 2 of the 7 came by 32. The 85 points against New York are Milwaukee's fewest in a game this season; the previous low was 97, the night before, also against Boston.

## The 2nd 7-game skid in 12 months

This is Milwaukee's 2nd 7-game skid in 12 months. The last one ran last November, in a season the Bucks finished 32-50, and those 7 losses came by 11.7 a game. Before last November, Milwaukee had gone 11 seasons without a 7-game skid; the longest in that span was 6. The current skid is worse by 8.6 points a game.

The losing is not new, but the scale of it is. A year ago the skid came with a team that still won 32 games; this one comes with a team that has won 1 of 11. Milwaukee's point differential is -14.9 a game, the 2nd-worst in the NBA behind Chicago's -16.6.

New York, which held Milwaukee to 85, allows 102.3 points a game, the fewest in the NBA. The Bucks scored 85 against the league's stingiest defense, but the 7-game skid is not a one-night story: 5 of the 7 losses came by 14 or more, and the one win in 11 games came by 8.
