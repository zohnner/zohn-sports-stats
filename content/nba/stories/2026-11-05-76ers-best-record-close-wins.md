The [Philadelphia 76ers](/nba/standings) are 8-1, the best record in the NBA, per ESPN's standings. The [Los Angeles Lakers](/nba/standings) and the [Houston Rockets](/nba/standings) are next at 7-2. But Philadelphia's point differential is plus-5.8 per game, 9th of the league's 30 teams.

## Close wins, not blowouts

The 8 win margins, in order: 6, 8, 11, 5, 5, 5, 19, 6. 7 of the 8 wins came by 11 points or fewer, and 5 by 6 or fewer. Only the 19-point win over Chicago was comfortable, and the latest win came at Detroit, 112-106, against the team that still allows the fewest points in the league, 101.8 a game. Philadelphia's 1 loss came by 13 points, at home to Detroit.

## Who they have beaten

The 8 wins came against opponents who are now a combined 26-40. 3 of the 8 wins came by 5 points, and 2 more by 6. The record is real, but it was built against a losing slate, and it leans on close games breaking Philadelphia's way.

Through 9 games, Philadelphia has the league's best record and its 9th-best differential. The differential says this is a good team winning close games, not a great team rolling opponents. The record says 8-1. Both are true, and the gap between them is the story.
