The [Minnesota Timberwolves](/nba/team/min) outscore opponents by 13.4 points a game, the best differential in the NBA, per ESPN's standings. Boston and San Antonio are next at plus-11.2 each. Minnesota scores 119.3 a game and allows 105.9.

The lead is new. Minnesota was at plus-11.1 a game yesterday, 3rd in the league, and the 39-point win over the [Dallas Mavericks](/nba/team/dal), 133-94 on Thursday, lifted it past both teams in a single night.

The streak itself is the mechanism: 5 straight wins by 16.4 points a game on average. Minnesota is 9-3.

The schedule tempers it. 3 of the 5 wins came against teams under .500, including 1-11 Milwaukee and 1-11 Utah, so the streak ran through soft opposition. And 12 games is early: the lead over Boston and San Antonio is 2.2 points a game.
