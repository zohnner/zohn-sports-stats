**Title:** Quentin Grimes scored 47 with 10 threes, joining Kobe as the only Lakers ever to do it

Quentin Grimes scored a career-high 47 points, including 21 in the 4th quarter, as the Lakers rallied from 18 down to beat the Hornets 118-114 in overtime for a 9th straight win. His 10 threes make him the 2nd Laker ever with 10 in a game, after Kobe Bryant's 12. Luka Doncic added 36, and the Lakers are 13-2 with the NBA's best record.
