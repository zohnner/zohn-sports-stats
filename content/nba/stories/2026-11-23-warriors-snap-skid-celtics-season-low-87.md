[Golden State Warriors](/nba/team/gsw) beat the [Boston Celtics](/nba/team/bos) 88-87 on Sunday, ending a 5-game losing streak. Boston's 87 points are its fewest in 17 games this season; the Celtics' previous low was 93, against Houston.

All 5 losses in the skid came on the road: at Houston, at San Antonio, at New Orleans, at Dallas and at Memphis. Golden State's last win before the skid came at home against New Orleans.

[Brandin Podziemski](/nba/player/4709138/brandin-podziemski) led Golden State with 18 points. [Jayson Tatum](/nba/player/4065648/jayson-tatum) had 27 points and 10 rebounds for Boston, but the Celtics shot 39% from the field and 30% from 3-point range, and scored 12 points in the fourth quarter. Golden State won despite 19 turnovers to Boston's 14.

Golden State is 9-8; Boston fell to 11-6.
