The [Houston Rockets](/nba/team/hou/houston-rockets) beat the [Washington Wizards](/nba/team/wsh/washington-wizards) 147-119 on Sunday, the most points any NBA team has scored this season. The previous high was 145, done 2 times this season, and Houston's own previous high was 132, against Milwaukee.

The 147 came without a 30-point scorer. Amen Thompson led Houston with 26 points on 9-of-11 shooting, Kevin Durant and Tari Eason scored 25 each, and Alperen Sengun had 19 points, 8 rebounds and 8 assists. 6 Rockets reached double figures, and Houston shot 58 percent from the field with 40 assists, and led by as many as 37.

Washington allowed 147, the most it has allowed this season — the previous high was 144, against Detroit — and Trae Young went scoreless, missing all 5 of his shots in 26 minutes. Houston is 11-3 with 4 straight wins, and the 147 moved its scoring average to 118.4 points a game.
