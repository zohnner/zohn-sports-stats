**Title:** The Timberwolves lead the NBA in point differential at plus-13.4 a game after winning 5 straight by 16.4 a game

Minnesota was 3rd in the league at plus-11.1 a game yesterday. A 39-point rout of Dallas, 133-94, lifted the Timberwolves past Boston and San Antonio, who sit at plus-11.2 each. Minnesota scores 119.3 a game and allows 105.9.

The 5-game win streak has come by 12, 10, 6, 15 and 39 points. The schedule tempers it: 3 of the 5 wins came against teams under .500, including 1-11 Milwaukee and 1-11 Utah. Minnesota is 9-3.
