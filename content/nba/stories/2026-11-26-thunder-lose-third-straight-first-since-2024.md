The [Minnesota Timberwolves](/nba/team/min) beat the [Oklahoma City Thunder](/nba/team/okc) 113-106 at the Paycom Center on Wednesday, handing Oklahoma City a 3rd straight loss. The Thunder had not lost 3 straight since April 2024, a span of 165 games across the last 2 seasons with no 3-game skid.

Oklahoma City lost 123-113 to Miami, 125-120 to Indiana, and now 113-106 to Minnesota. In 2024-25 the Thunder played 83 games and never lost more than 2 straight; in 2025-26 they played 82 and never lost more than 2 straight. Their last 3-game losing streak was April 2-5, 2024, at Philadelphia, Boston and Indiana. Oklahoma City is 10-9.

[Jaden McDaniels](/nba/player/4431671/jaden-mcdaniels) led Minnesota with 32 points and 10 rebounds on 11-of-15 shooting, including 4 of 5 from 3-point range, 2 nights after his career-high 34 against Brooklyn. His 2 best scoring nights of the season came 2 nights apart; his previous best was 25 on Nov 21. [Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](/nba/player/4278073/shai-gilgeous-alexander) scored 32 for Oklahoma City on 13-of-26 shooting.

Minnesota is 14-5 with a 3rd straight win, and its plus-10.4 point differential is the 2nd-best in the NBA, behind San Antonio's plus-11.3. Oklahoma City's differential is plus-2.6.
