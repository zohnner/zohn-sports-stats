The [Milwaukee Bucks](/nba/team/mil) are 1-14, worse through 15 games than the team that finished 15-67, the worst record in franchise history. That team was 2-13 through 15 games; this one is a game worse. The 137-130 loss at Miami on Wednesday was the 11th straight defeat, and Milwaukee has been outscored by 197 points across the 15 games.

The skid is 11 games long, matching the 15-67 team's longest skid of 11. Milwaukee's only win came against Indiana, 110-102; every game since has been a loss, by 15.9 points a game on average.

The shape of the skid is changing, even if the results are not. The first 7 losses came by 20.3 points a game; the last 4 have come by 11 points or fewer. Milwaukee is scoring 107.7 points a game and allowing 120.9, and the 130 points at Miami were its most in a loss all season.
