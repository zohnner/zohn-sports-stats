[LeBron James](/nba/player/1966/lebron-james) recorded his first triple-double of the season, 25 points, 10 rebounds and 12 assists, as the [Philadelphia 76ers](/nba/team/phi) beat the [Miami Heat](/nba/team/mia) 111-104 at the Xfinity Mobile Arena on Wednesday. Philadelphia is 15-4, the best record in the East.

James had played 14 games without a triple-double before Wednesday. The 25 points are his season high, after 23 against Portland on Monday, and the 12 assists are also a season high, after 11 against Indiana on Nov 15. [Tyrese Maxey](/nba/player/4431678/tyrese-maxey) led Philadelphia with 30 points on 12-of-21 shooting.

Miami lost despite 32 points and 14 rebounds from Giannis Antetokounmpo, who shot 11 of 18 from the field. The Heat are 10-7. Philadelphia has won 15 of its first 19 games; Boston and New York are next in the East at 12-6 each.
