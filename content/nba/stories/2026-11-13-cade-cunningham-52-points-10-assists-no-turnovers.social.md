**Title:** Cade Cunningham is the first player in NBA history with 50 points, 10 assists and 0 turnovers in a game

Cunningham scored a career-high 52 points with 11 assists and 0 turnovers in Detroit's 144-140 double-overtime win over Washington. He had 22 points through 3 quarters, then scored 30 in the 4th quarter and the overtimes — the most by a Pistons player in the play-by-play era.

The 52 ties George Yardley's 52 for the 4th-highest scoring game in Pistons history. Ausar Thompson added his 1st career triple-double with 17 points, 11 rebounds and 11 assists. Detroit is 8-3.
