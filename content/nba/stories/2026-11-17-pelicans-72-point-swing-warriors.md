The [New Orleans Pelicans](/nba/team/no) lost to the [Golden State Warriors](/nba/team/gs) by 51 points last week. 7 days later, they beat the same team by 21, 115-94 in New Orleans on Monday — a 72-point swing in the space of a week.

The win ended a 6-game losing streak for New Orleans, a run that included the 51-point loss itself. For Golden State, it was a 3rd straight loss, after a 115-108 loss at Houston and a 123-85 loss at San Antonio, by a combined 66 points. The Warriors had won 8 straight before the slide.

[Jeremiah Fears](/nba/player/5144091/jeremiah-fears) posted his first career triple-double in the win — 19 points, 10 rebounds and 10 assists in 29 minutes — and [Saddiq Bey](/nba/player/4397136/saddiq-bey) scored 23 points. Golden State shot 28 of 84 from the field (33%) and 11 of 46 from 3-point range (24%), and [Yaxel Lendeborg](/nba/player/5175737/yaxel-lendeborg) led the Warriors with 28 points.
