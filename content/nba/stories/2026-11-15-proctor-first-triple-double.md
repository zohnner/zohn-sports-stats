[Tyrese Proctor](/nba/player/5023693/tyrese-proctor) recorded his first career triple-double on Saturday: 24 points, 11 rebounds and 10 assists in the [Cleveland Cavaliers](/nba/team/cle)' 127-117 win over the [Dallas Mavericks](/nba/team/dal). He shot 10 of 19 from the field in 34 minutes.

The win moved Cleveland to 7-7. Dallas fell to 7-6 despite 34 points from Kyrie Irving on 11-of-25 shooting, and Proctor had 10 assists against 1 turnover.

One triple-double does not make a season, and Cleveland is 7-7. But a first career triple-double is the kind of night that sticks in a young guard's record, and this one came in a win over a winning team.
