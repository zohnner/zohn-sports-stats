[Luka Doncic](/nba/player/3945274/luka-doncic) is averaging 40.1 points per game through 9 games, the most in the NBA, per ESPN's leaders. No NBA player has opened a season at 40 points a game through 9 games since Wilt Chamberlain in the 1962-63 season.

## 53 against Portland, and 6 40-point games in 9

Doncic scored 53 points against Portland on Friday night, making 17 of 33 shots from the field, as the [Los Angeles Lakers](/nba/standings) won 124-118. It was his 6th game of 40 or more in 9 games, and his 4th in a row.

His 9 games, in order: 28, 23, 52, 41, 37, 45, 42, 40, 53. After 28 and 23 to open the season, he has scored at least 37 in each game since, 7 straight games. The 52 came at Utah, the 45 against Toronto, the 42 against Miami, and the 40 at Sacramento.

## The last 40-point start was Wilt's

Chamberlain scored 472 points in his first 9 games of the 1962-63 season, an average of 52.4, on his way to 44.8 for the season. Since then, the best 9-game starts by the game's great scorers all fall short of 40: Michael Jordan averaged 38.6 through 9 games in the 1986-87 season, Rick Barry 37.2 in the 1966-67 season, James Harden 37.1 in the 2019-20 season.

The check covered every season in which a player averaged 33 points a game or more, plus the famous hot starts — [Stephen Curry](/nba/player/3975/stephen-curry) in the 2015-16 season, Trae Young in the 2019-20 season, Giannis Antetokounmpo in the 2022-23 season, Damian Lillard in the 2022-23 season, Kobe Bryant in the 2006-07 season, Kevin Durant in the 2013-14 season, Tracy McGrady in the 2002-03 season, Bernard King in the 1984-85 season — and none of them reached 40 through 9 games.

## What the scoring has not done

The Lakers are 8-2, and Doncic's 361 points are 29.6% of their 1,220 points this season. The average will move — 9 games is a small slice of a season, and no player has finished a season averaging 40 since Chamberlain's 44.8 in the 1962-63 season. But through 9 games, the scoring lead is not a rounding error: it is wider than the spread across the next 5 scorers, and it grew with every 40-point night.
