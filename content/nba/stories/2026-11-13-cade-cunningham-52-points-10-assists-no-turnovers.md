[Cade Cunningham](/nba/player/4432166/cade-cunningham) scored a career-high 52 points with 11 assists and 0 turnovers in the [Detroit Pistons](/nba/team/det)' 144-140 double-overtime win over the [Washington Wizards](/nba/team/wsh) on Thursday, the first 50-point, 10-assist, 0-turnover game since the NBA began tracking turnovers in the 1977-78 season, per OptaSTATS.

His previous career high was 46 points, also against Washington, just over a year ago. He had 22 points through 3 quarters, then scored 18 in the 4th and 12 more across the overtimes — 30 in the 4th quarter and overtime combined, the most by a Pistons player in the play-by-play era, which began in the 1997-98 season.

He shot 16 of 25 from the field, 3 of 5 from 3-point range and 17 of 18 at the line in 48 minutes, adding 5 rebounds, 1 steal and 1 block. The 52 ties George Yardley's 52 in the 1957-58 season for the 4th-highest scoring game in Pistons history, behind Jerry Stackhouse's 57 in the 2000-01 season, Kelly Tripucka's 56 in the 1982-83 season and Dave Bing's 54 in the 1970-71 season.

He is the 5th player with a 50-point double-double and 0 turnovers since the 1977-78 season, joining Michael Jordan, Moses Malone, Carmelo Anthony and Giannis Antetokounmpo, and the 3rd Pistons player with a 50-point double-double, after George Yardley and Blake Griffin.

## Thompson's night inside Cunningham's

[Ausar Thompson](/nba/player/4684742/ausar-thompson) added his 1st career triple-double with 17 points, 11 rebounds and 11 assists. Trae Young had 37 points and 14 assists for Washington, and Anthony Davis and Alex Sarr scored 30 each — the franchise's first trio of 30-point scorers since 1969. Washington has lost 6 straight and is 4-9. Detroit is 8-3.

The record covers the turnover era, which began in the 1977-78 season — turnovers were not tracked before that, so the comparison cannot reach further back. And it is 1 game: a career night, not a new baseline. Cunningham averages 7.9 assists a game, 5th in the NBA. The Pistons visit Miami on Saturday.
