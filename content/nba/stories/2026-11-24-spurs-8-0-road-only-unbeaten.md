The [San Antonio Spurs](/nba/team/sas) are 8-0 on the road, the only unbeaten road team in the NBA. They stayed perfect away from home with a 123-107 win at the [Sacramento Kings](/nba/team/sac) on Monday, and the next-best road records are Memphis at 9-1, and the Lakers and 76ers at 7-1.

San Antonio did it without Victor Wembanyama, De'Aaron Fox and Dylan Harper in the lineup. [Stephon Castle](/nba/player/4845367/stephon-castle) scored 28 points with 8 rebounds and 11 assists, [Julian Champagnie](/nba/player/4592479/julian-champagnie) added 22, and [Luke Kornet](/nba/player/3064560/luke-kornet) had 18 points and 11 rebounds.

The Spurs trailed 28-19 after the first quarter, then outscored Sacramento 41-23 in the next quarter and never trailed again.

San Antonio's 8 road wins have come by 16, 6, 15, 22, 26, 5, 8 and 16 points, an average of 14.3 a game. The Spurs are 7-3 at home, so they have been better on the road than at home, and their plus-219 point differential is the best in the NBA.

The Spurs are 15-3, tied with the Lakers for the league's best record.
