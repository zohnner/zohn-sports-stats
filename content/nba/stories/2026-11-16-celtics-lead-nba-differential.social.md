**Title:** Boston now leads the NBA in point differential at plus-13.1 a game

The Celtics beat Dallas 131-94 on Sunday, a 37-point win that moved Boston past San Antonio into the league lead in point differential (plus-13.1, ahead of the Spurs' plus-12.9). Boston also allows a league-low 102.4 points a game. Tatum scored 32.
