The [Portland Trail Blazers](/nba/team/por) beat the [San Antonio Spurs](/nba/team/sa) 116-111 at the Moda Center on Wednesday, 2 nights after beating the [Philadelphia 76ers](/nba/team/phi) 128-111. The 2 teams Portland beat were a combined 29-6: Philadelphia was 14-3, and San Antonio was 15-3. Portland is 12-6.

The win ended the Spurs' perfect road record. San Antonio was 8-0 away from home, the NBA's only unbeaten road team, and is now 8-1 on the road and 15-4 overall. Philadelphia fell to 14-4 with the loss, then beat Miami 111-104 on Wednesday to reach 15-4.

[Deni Avdija](/nba/player/4683021/deni-avdija) led Portland with 33 points, 12 rebounds and 7 assists, and [Damian Lillard](/nba/player/6606/damian-lillard) added 29 points. Avdija's 33 is his 2nd-highest scoring night of the season, after 34 on Nov 13, and his 12 rebounds tie his season high. Portland made 19 3-pointers on 50 attempts.

[Victor Wembanyama](/nba/player/5104157/victor-wembanyama) had 44 points and 16 rebounds for San Antonio in the loss, his 3rd-highest scoring night of the season after 52 on Nov 4 and 50 on Nov 21. The Spurs shot 10 of 36 from 3-point range.
