The [Tampa Bay Buccaneers](/nfl/team/tb) are 3-6, yet they have outscored their opponents 202-178 through 9 games, a plus-24 point differential, per ESPN's standings.

Since 1999, only 2 teams have gone 3-6 through 9 games with a plus-20 differential or better, per nflverse. Tampa Bay is one of them, at plus-24; the other is the [Jacksonville Jaguars](/nfl/team/jax) in the 2022 season, at plus-21. The comparison counts only teams that played all 9 games: a team coming off its bye with 8 played can sit better on fewer wins — the [Houston Texans](/nfl/team/hou) were 3-5 with a plus-47 differential at this point last season.

The record and the differential disagree because every loss has been close. Tampa Bay lost 27-33 to the [Cincinnati Bengals](/nfl/team/cin), 19-23 to the [Cleveland Browns](/nfl/team/cle), 16-23 to the [Minnesota Vikings](/nfl/team/min), 14-17 to the [Green Bay Packers](/nfl/team/gb), 19-26 to the [Pittsburgh Steelers](/nfl/team/pit) and 24-27 to the [Chicago Bears](/nfl/team/chi): 6 losses by 7 points or fewer, an average margin of defeat of 5.0 points.

Game by game, the shape is the same — small losses, big wins:

The 3 wins add up to plus-54; the 6 losses add up to minus-30. The differential is plus-24 because the wins have been blowouts and the losses have been coin flips.

The defense is what keeps the differential positive. Tampa Bay allows 19.8 points per game against a league average of 23.0, while scoring 22.4 per game — below average, but not by enough to erase what the defense saves. Tampa Bay is also 2-0 inside the NFC South, the only unbeaten division record in the South, per ESPN's standings.

The only precedent turned it around. Jacksonville started 3-6 with a plus-21 differential in the 2022 season and finished 9-8, winning 6 of its final 8 games. That is a precedent, not a promise: Tampa Bay has still lost 6 games, and a plus-24 differential pays nothing in the standings.
