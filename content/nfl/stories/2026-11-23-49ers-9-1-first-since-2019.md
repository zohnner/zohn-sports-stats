[San Francisco 49ers](/nfl/team/sf) beat the [Minnesota Vikings](/nfl/team/min) 35-10 on Sunday in Mexico City, moving to 9-1. It is San Francisco's first 9-1 start since 2019, and only the 3rd 9-1 start through 10 games for the franchise since 1999 — the others came in 2011 and 2019.

Brock Purdy completed 21 of 26 passes for 255 yards and 3 touchdowns with no interceptions, a 146.0 passer rating. San Francisco scored 21 points in the second quarter and led 28-10 at halftime.

Minnesota scored nothing in the second half. The Vikings gained 324 total yards to San Francisco's 343 but turned the ball over 2 times to the 49ers' 0. Christian McCaffrey ran for 49 yards and a touchdown.

The game drew 85,612 fans at Estadio Banorte in Mexico City. San Francisco's only loss this season came at Atlanta in Week 7.
