Through Week 6, the [Cleveland Browns](/nfl/team/cle) are 4-2 despite being outscored 122-106, per SportStrata's standings data. That 16-point gap is the worst differential of any team with a winning record — Denver, the next-closest at 4-2, sits at minus-11.

Game by game: lost 34-10 to Jacksonville, won 23-19 over Tampa Bay, 21-18 over Carolina, 27-24 over Pittsburgh, lost 17-12 to the Jets, and beat Baltimore 13-10. All 4 wins came in one-score games, by 4, 3, 3, and 3 points — 13 combined. The 2 losses came by 24 and 5 — 29 combined. None of the winning has been comfortable; none of the losing has been close.

The skeptic's case: 4-2 is 4-2, and winning close games is a skill. Fair. But Cleveland's 106 points are the fewest of any team with a winning record (Minnesota has the next-fewest at 109), and no winning team has been outscored by more.

The caveat: 6 games is 6 games. Maybe Cleveland is just clutch. But no winning team is living closer to the edge — 4 wins by 13 combined points is a thin margin to keep threading.
