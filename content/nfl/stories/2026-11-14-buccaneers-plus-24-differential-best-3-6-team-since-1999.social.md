**Title:** Tampa Bay's plus-24 differential is the best for a 3-6 team since 1999

Tampa Bay is 3-6 with a plus-24 point differential through 9 games — the best of any team 3-6 or worse through 9 games since 1999. Only 2 teams since 1999 have gone 3-6 through 9 games with a plus-20 differential or better: Tampa Bay at plus-24 and Jacksonville in the 2022 season at plus-21. (Teams coming off a bye with 8 played can sit better — Houston was 3-5 with a plus-47 differential at this point last season.)

All 6 of Tampa Bay's losses came by 7 points or fewer, an average of 5.0 points. The 3 wins came by an average of 18.0 points. The defense allows 19.8 points per game against a league average of 23.0.
