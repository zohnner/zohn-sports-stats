[Buffalo Bills](/nfl/team/buf) beat the [New York Jets](/nfl/team/nyj) 31-12 on Sunday to move to 8-1, the best record in the AFC and tied with San Francisco for the best in the NFL. Per nflverse, which tracks every game since 1999, Buffalo had never started 8-1 or better through 9 games before this season.

The Bills have scored 287 points, the most in the NFL — 31.9 a game — one more than Dallas at 286. Their differential is plus-88, 2nd in the league behind Jacksonville's plus-125. They have won 5 straight since a 26-29 loss to New England in Week 4, the last 3 by 26, 17 and 19 points.

The one loss is the note of caution: it came against a 5-5 New England team, and 3 of the 8 wins came against teams now 3-6 or worse. But an 8-1 start is an 8-1 start, and no Bills team in the last 28 seasons has had one.
