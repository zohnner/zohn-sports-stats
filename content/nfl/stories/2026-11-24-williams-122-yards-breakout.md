[Washington Commanders](/nfl/team/wsh) receiver [Antonio Williams](/nfl/player/5081432/antonio-williams) caught 9 passes for 122 yards in Monday's 22-20 win over the [Cincinnati Bengals](/nfl/team/cin), both season highs. His previous bests were 5 catches and 64 yards, and the 122 yards were 37.4% of the 326 yards he had totaled in his first 9 games.

[Jayden Daniels](/nfl/player/4426348/jayden-daniels) threw to Williams 11 times, and Williams caught 9 of them, including a 42-yard catch. Daniels finished 23 of 35 for 289 yards, and his 344 total yards, counting 55 on the ground, are his most in a game this season; his previous best was 310, in Week 6.

Cincinnati led 20-19 with 46 seconds left after Evan McPherson's 56-yard field goal, but Daniels drove Washington to Drew Stevens' 26-yard field goal as time expired. Stevens made all 5 of his field-goal attempts.

The win moved Washington to 4-5-1. Cincinnati fell to 7-3.
