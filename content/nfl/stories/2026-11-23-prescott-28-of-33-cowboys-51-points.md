[Dallas Cowboys](/nfl/team/dal) scored 51 points against the [Tennessee Titans](/nfl/team/ten) on Sunday, the most Dallas has scored in 11 games this season. The previous high was 40, against San Francisco a week earlier.

Dak Prescott completed 28 of 33 passes for 325 yards and 4 touchdowns with no interceptions, a 147.3 passer rating. His 84.8% completion rate is his best of the season; his previous best was 83.9% against Washington in Week 2, when he went 26 of 31.

CeeDee Lamb caught 11 passes for 132 yards and a touchdown, and George Pickens caught 7 for 102 yards and a touchdown. It is the first game this season in which Dallas had 2 receivers reach 100 yards; in the other 10 games, the Cowboys had at most 1.

Dallas gained 480 total yards to Tennessee's 346 and committed no turnovers to the Titans' 1. Dallas scored in every quarter — 13, 14, 14 and 10 — while Tennessee's Cam Ward threw for 248 yards and 2 touchdowns with no interceptions.

The win moved Dallas to 5-6. Tennessee fell to 1-9.
