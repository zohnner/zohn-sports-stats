**Title:** The Colts scored 3 points, their fewest in a game since December 2022

Indianapolis managed 167 yards and 8 first downs in a 26-3 loss at Houston on Thursday — 1 of 11 on third down, 12 of 29 passing. The Colts had scored at least 7 in every game this season before Thursday. Houston has now held 2 opponents to 3 points this season, both by 26-3. A 3-point game isn't rare on its own (8 of 151 games this season), but it's the Colts' fewest since a 20-3 loss in December 2022.
