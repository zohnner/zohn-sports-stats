Through 8 games, the [Minnesota Vikings](/nfl/team/min) are 6-2 and have scored 146 points, 18.3 a game. That is the fewest points any team 6-2 or better has scored through 8 games since 1999, per nflverse. The 76 teams that started 6-2 or better in that span all scored more; the previous low was 149, by the 2000 Washington team that also started 6-2 and finished 8-8. The league average this season is 23.1 points a game.

## The defense is the whole story

Minnesota has allowed 116 points, the fewest of the league's 32 teams, or 14.5 a game. Jacksonville is next at 121. The Vikings have held 5 of their 8 opponents to 16 points or fewer.

The schedule is not doing the work. Minnesota's 8 opponents are a combined 35-33, and those teams average 25.3 points in their other games, against 14.5 against the Vikings, a gap of 10.8 points a game.

## The offense has not scored more than 23 since Week 1

Minnesota scored 39 against Green Bay in the opener and has not topped 23 in the 7 games since. In the 2 losses, Minnesota scored 15 and 6 points. Buffalo's 23 points on Monday night were the most the Vikings have allowed all season.

Minnesota is 4th-lowest in scoring in the league. Only the 0-8 Chargers with 121 points, 1-7 Miami with 126 and 1-7 Tennessee with 144 have scored fewer. Those 3 teams are a combined 2-22. The Vikings are 6-2 because the defense gives them room for an offense that scores 18.3 a game.
