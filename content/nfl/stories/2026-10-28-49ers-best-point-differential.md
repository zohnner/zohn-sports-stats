The [San Francisco 49ers](/nfl/team/sf) have outscored their opponents 197-139 through Week 7, a plus-58 differential that leads the NFL's 32 teams, per ESPN's standings. Minnesota is next at plus-51, and Jacksonville at plus-50.

## Game by game

- Week 1: won 27-7 at [Los Angeles Rams](/nfl/team/lar)
- Week 2: won 35-13 vs [Miami](/nfl/team/mia)
- Week 3: won 36-30 vs [Arizona](/nfl/team/ari)
- Week 4: won 24-14 vs [Denver](/nfl/team/den)
- Week 5: won 32-29 at [Seattle](/nfl/team/sea)
- Week 6: won 23-17 vs [Washington](/nfl/team/wsh)
- Week 7: lost 20-29 at [Atlanta](/nfl/team/atl)

## Where the margin comes from

San Francisco scores 28.1 points per game and allows 19.9, against a league average of 23.2. The 6 wins came by 20, 22, 6, 10, 3, and 6 points, and the only loss came by 9.

## Not just a soft schedule

The skeptic's case is the schedule: San Francisco's 7 opponents are a combined 19-27. But Minnesota, next at plus-51, faced opponents a combined 16-25, a softer slate than San Francisco's.

## The caveat

The caveat: 7 games is 7 games. San Francisco's only loss came in Week 7, and the lead over Minnesota is 7 points, so 1 game can erase it.
