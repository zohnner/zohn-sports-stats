The [Denver Broncos](/nfl/team/den) are 7-2, and they have outscored their opponents by 12 points, 198-186, per ESPN's standings. Among the league's 7-win teams, it is the thinnest margin by far: Chicago is next at plus-57, and San Francisco sits at plus-79.

## One of 3 since 1999

Since the 1999 season, 3 teams have won 7 of their first 9 games while outscoring opponents by 12 points or fewer, per nflverse: Atlanta of the 2004 season at plus-10, Buffalo of the 2020 season at plus-9, and Denver now. The other 2 finished 11-5 and 13-3.

## Denver wins close and loses big

6 of the 7 wins came by 7 points or fewer, and the 7 wins came by 43 points combined. The 2 losses, 31-10 to Kansas City and 24-14 to San Francisco, came by 31 points combined.
