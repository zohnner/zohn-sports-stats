**Title:** The 49ers are 9-1 for the first time since 2019

Brock Purdy completed 21 of 26 passes for 255 yards and 3 touchdowns with no interceptions, a 146.0 passer rating, as San Francisco beat Minnesota 35-10 in Mexico City. It is the 49ers' first 9-1 start since 2019, and only the 3rd since 1999. Minnesota scored nothing in the second half, and the game drew 85,612 fans at Estadio Banorte.
