**Title:** The Chargers won their first game after an 0-8 start — only the 6th 0-8 team since 1999 to win its 9th game

Los Angeles beat Baltimore 23-20 on Monday for its first win of the season. Since 1999, 18 teams have started 0-8, and only 6 won their 9th game — the Chargers are the first since 2013. Omarion Hampton ran for a season-high 105 yards, and the Chargers held Derrick Henry to 10 yards on 10 carries. History is still unkind: of the 17 teams before them to start 0-8, only 1 finished better than 4-12.
