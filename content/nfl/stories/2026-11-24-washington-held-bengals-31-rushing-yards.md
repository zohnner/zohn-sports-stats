The [Washington Commanders](/nfl/team/wsh) held the [Cincinnati Bengals](/nfl/team/cin) to 31 rushing yards in Monday's 22-20 win, the fewest Washington has allowed in a game this season. The previous low was 44, against Seattle in Week 3.

Chase Brown, Cincinnati's lead back, ran 6 times for 6 yards, and Samaje Perine added 26 yards on 6 carries. Joe Burrow was sacked 3 times for 19 yards, and his 2 scrambles lost yardage.

Cincinnati still threw for 300 yards, with Burrow completing 28 of 40 passes for 2 touchdowns, but the Bengals never found a run game: their longest run was Perine's 11-yard gain.

The win moved Washington to 4-5-1. Cincinnati fell to 7-3.
