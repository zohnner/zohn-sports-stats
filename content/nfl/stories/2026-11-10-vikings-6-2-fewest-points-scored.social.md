**Title:** The 6-2 Vikings have scored 146 points, the fewest of any 6-2 team since 1999 — and allowed 116, the fewest in the NFL

Minnesota is 6-2 while scoring 18.3 points a game, against a league average of 23.1. No team 6-2 or better through 8 games has scored fewer since 1999; the previous low was 149, by the 2000 Washington team that finished 8-8.

The defense is the whole story: 116 points allowed, the fewest in the league, and 5 of 8 opponents held to 16 or fewer. The schedule is not doing the work — those opponents are a combined 35-33 and average 25.3 points in their other games, against 14.5 against Minnesota.

Minnesota scored 39 in the Week 1 opener and has not topped 23 in the 7 games since.
