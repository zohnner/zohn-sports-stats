**Title:** The Eagles are 2-7, the 6th team since 1999 to lose 7 straight after 2-0

Philadelphia won its first 2 games, then lost 7 straight — 100 points scored against 179 allowed in the skid. Since the 1999 season, only 6 teams have started 2-0 and then lost 7 in a row, and none of the previous 5 finished with more than 5 wins. The latest loss was 19-11 at home to the Giants on Sunday.
