[San Francisco 49ers](/nfl/team/sf) beat the [Dallas Cowboys](/nfl/team/dal) 46-40 on Sunday in the 2nd-highest-scoring game of the season — 86 points, behind only the 96 in Chicago's 59-37 win over Carolina in Week 1. Dallas became only the 26th team since 1999 to score 40 points and lose, and the first to do it this season.

Christian McCaffrey drove the 49ers' 46, the most San Francisco has scored this season: 3 touchdowns and 223 yards from scrimmage — 161 receiving on 4 catches, 62 rushing — while Brock Purdy threw for 389 yards and 3 touchdowns with no interceptions. Dak Prescott threw 5 touchdown passes for Dallas, and George Pickens caught 3 of them for 145 yards, and it still was not enough.

Dallas has now scored 286 points, the 2nd-most in the NFL, and allowed 282, the 3rd-most — the Cowboys' problem is not the offense. San Francisco is 8-1, tied with Buffalo for the NFL's best record, and the 46 was its season high by 8 over the 38 it scored against Las Vegas.
