The [Los Angeles Chargers](/nfl/team/lac) are no longer winless. They beat the [Baltimore Ravens](/nfl/team/bal) 23-20 in Baltimore on Monday, their first win after 8 straight losses to open the season. Since 1999, 18 teams have started 0-8, and the Chargers are only the 6th to win their 9th game — and the first to do it since 2013, when Jacksonville and Tampa Bay both did.

The win was built on the ground and on the ball. Omarion Hampton ran for a season-high 105 yards on 22 carries with 1 touchdown, and Justin Herbert added 51 rushing yards and a touchdown on 7 carries while throwing for 129 yards on 12 of 18 passing. On the other side, Los Angeles held Derrick Henry to 10 yards on 10 carries — his fewest rushing yards in a game this season, 35 below his previous low of 45 — though Henry still scored 2 touchdowns. The Chargers committed 0 turnovers to Baltimore's 2, and Cameron Dicker made all 3 of his field goals for 11 points.

History says the first win is usually the last good news. Of the 17 teams before Los Angeles to start 0-8 since 1999, only 1 finished better than 4-12 — the 2017 49ers, who finished 6-10 — and 2 finished without a win, Detroit in 2008 and Cleveland in 2017. The 5 previous teams to win their 9th game finished 3-13, 4-12, 4-12, 4-12 and 4-12.

Baltimore falls to 4-6 despite outgaining Los Angeles 347 yards to 287, with Jacoby Brissett throwing for 340 yards on 33 of 49 passing. The Chargers are 1-8. The win ends the winless start; history says the climb from here is steep — only the 2017 49ers turned an 0-8 start into more than 4 wins.
