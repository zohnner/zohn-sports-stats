**Title:** The Vikings are 7-2 while scoring 176 points — the 4th-fewest by a 7-2 team since 1999

Minnesota beat Green Bay 30-10 on Sunday, its largest win at Lambeau since 1999, and is 7-2 having scored just 176 points in 9 games. Only the 2001 Steelers (155), 2006 Broncos (158) and 2000 Giants (168) won 7 of 9 while scoring fewer. The defense has allowed 126 points, the fewest in the NFL.
