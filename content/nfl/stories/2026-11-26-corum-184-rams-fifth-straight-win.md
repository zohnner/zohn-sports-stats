[Blake Corum](/nfl/team/lar) carried 32 times for 184 yards and a touchdown as the [Los Angeles Rams](/nfl/team/lar) beat the [Green Bay Packers](/nfl/team/gb) 26-13 at SoFi Stadium on Wednesday night, the Rams' 5th straight win. Los Angeles is 7-4, and Green Bay fell to 6-5.

Corum's 184 yards nearly doubled his previous best of 100, set in Week 10 against Arizona, and his 32 carries were 15 more than his previous high of 17. The 184 yards are the 2nd-most by any player in a game this season, behind Bijan Robinson's 194 in Week 3, also against Green Bay. Corum's 32 carries are the most by a Ram in a game this season; Kyren Williams' high is 16.

The run game carried an offense that barely threw. The Rams ran for 241 yards as a team, a season high after 202 in Week 10, while [Matthew Stafford](/nfl/team/lar) went 16 of 23 for 140 yards with no touchdowns and 1 interception. The 140 yards are Stafford's lowest passing total of the season; his previous low was 155 in Week 1. Williams added 12 carries for 46 yards and a touchdown.

Green Bay had no answer on the ground either way. The Packers ran for 55 yards and gained 244 total yards, turned the ball over 3 times, and went 2 of 8 on third down against the Rams' 8 of 15. Jordan Love went 17 of 29 for 198 yards with 1 touchdown and 2 interceptions.
