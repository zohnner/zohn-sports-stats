**Title:** All 4 NFC North teams are 6-2 or better through Week 9 — the first time any division has done that since 1999

Chicago leads at 7-2, Minnesota and Detroit are 6-2, and Green Bay is 6-3. Together they are 25-9. No division in the 27 seasons from 1999 through 2025 had all 4 teams at 6-2 or better at the end of Week 9 — the closest was a weakest team with 5 wins, which happened 6 times.

The twist: Chicago leads the division without winning in it. The Bears lost 9-3 to Minnesota in Week 2 and 31-7 at Green Bay in Week 5, and they are 1 of 3 teams since 1999 to reach 7-2 or better by the end of Week 9 without a division win. 7 division games remain, so the race will be decided inside the division.
