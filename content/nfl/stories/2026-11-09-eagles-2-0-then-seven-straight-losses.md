The [Philadelphia Eagles](/nfl/team/phi) are 2-7, and the 7-game losing streak they carry into Week 10 began the week after a 2-0 start, per ESPN's standings. Philadelphia is the 6th team since the 1999 season to win its first 2 games and then lose the next 7, per nflverse.

## The skid is a scoring collapse

The 7 losses came by a combined 79 points, 100 scored against 179 allowed. Philadelphia averages 14.3 points in the 7 defeats, after 24.0 a game in the 2 wins. The 2 opening wins came by 2 and 4 points, so the 2-0 start was never comfortable.

## The company is grim

The 5 previous teams all finished with losing records: Chicago of the 2002 season finished 4-12, San Francisco of the 2007 season finished 5-11, Houston of the 2013 season finished 2-14, Dallas of the 2015 season finished 4-12, and New Orleans of the 2024 season finished 5-12. None won more than 5 games. The streaks kept going for 3 of them: Chicago and San Francisco each lost an 8th straight, and Houston lost 14 in a row.

The latest loss came Sunday, 19-11 at home to the [New York Giants](/nfl/team/nyg), who are now 5-2. 3 of the 7 defeats came by 8 points or fewer, so the streak is not only blowouts — but 9 games is 9 games, and the offense has topped 20 points in 1 of the 7 losses.
