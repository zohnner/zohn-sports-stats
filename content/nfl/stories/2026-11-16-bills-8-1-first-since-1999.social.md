**Title:** Buffalo is 8-1 for the first time since 1999

The Bills beat the Jets 31-12 on Sunday to move to 8-1, tied with San Francisco for the best record in the NFL. Per nflverse, Buffalo had never started 8-1 or better through 9 games before this season. The Bills have scored 287 points, the most in the NFL.
