**Title:** The Patriots are 6-0 in games after a loss under Mike Vrabel

New England is 6-0 in games immediately following a loss since Mike Vrabel took over in the 2025 season. The league wins 45.4% of games after a loss since 1999 — at that rate, 6 in a row happens about 0.9% of the time.

New England has lost 7 games under Vrabel, and won the next game after 6 of them. The next test is Sunday against Detroit, 6-2, in Munich.
