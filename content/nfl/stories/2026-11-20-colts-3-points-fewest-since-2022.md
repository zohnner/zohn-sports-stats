The [Indianapolis Colts](/nfl/team/ind) scored 3 points in a 26-3 loss at the [Houston Texans](/nfl/team/hou) on Thursday, their fewest in a game since December 2022, when the Chargers beat them 20-3.

The offense never got going: 167 total yards, 8 first downs, 1 of 11 on third down, and 12 of 29 passing for 129 net yards, at 3.5 yards per play. Indianapolis had scored at least 7 points in every game this season before Thursday; its previous low was 7 points at Jacksonville. Through 11 games the Colts have scored 211 points.

Houston has now held 2 opponents to 3 points this season — Tennessee last month, by the same 26-3 score, and Indianapolis on Thursday. The Texans are 4-6, but their defense has produced the 2 lowest totals of the Colts' season: 3 points on Thursday, after 7 at Jacksonville.

A 3-point game is not rare on its own: 8 of the 151 games played so far this season have had a team score 3 or fewer, including one shutout. What makes Thursday stand out is whose offense it was. Indianapolis falls to 3-8, and the 3 points are its fewest since a 20-3 loss in December 2022.
