**Title:** The 7-2 Broncos are plus-12, one of 3 such starts since 1999

Denver is 7-2 but has outscored opponents by just 12 points, 198-186 — the thinnest margin of any 7-win team. Since 1999, only 3 teams have won 7 of their first 9 while outscoring opponents by 12 or fewer: Atlanta of the 2004 season, Buffalo of the 2020 season, and Denver now. 6 of the 7 wins came by 7 points or fewer.
