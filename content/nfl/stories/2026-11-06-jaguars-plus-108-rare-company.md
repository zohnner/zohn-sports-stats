The [Jacksonville Jaguars](/nfl/team/jax) have outscored their opponents 229-121 through 8 games, a plus-108 differential that leads the NFL's 32 teams, per ESPN's standings. The [San Francisco 49ers](/nfl/team/sf) are next at plus-58, a 50-point gap. Jacksonville scores 28.6 points per game and allows 15.1, against a league average of 23.2. Thursday's 33-10 win at Baltimore made it 6-2.

## How rare is plus-108

Since the 1999 season, 26 of the 880 team-seasons that reached 8 games posted a differential of plus-108 or better, per nflverse. The biggest belongs to the [New England Patriots](/nfl/team/ne) of the 2007 season, at plus-204, followed by the Patriots of the 2019 season at plus-189. The most recent team before Jacksonville was the [Indianapolis Colts](/nfl/team/ind) of the 2025 season, at plus-116, and the [Detroit Lions](/nfl/team/det) of the 2024 season reached plus-110. Jacksonville is the 3rd team in 3 seasons to do it.

The last Jaguars team to start this way was the 1999 season team, at plus-134 through 8 games, which finished 14-2. No Jaguars team since has reached plus-108 through 8 games until this one.
