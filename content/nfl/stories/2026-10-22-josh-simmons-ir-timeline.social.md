**Title:** Josh Simmons hit IR 66 days after the Chiefs found his herniated disc

On Thursday, Chiefs VP of sports medicine Rick Burkholder laid out the full file for the first time: calf discomfort in mid-August, an MRI that found a bulging herniated disc, a specialist who recommended rehab over surgery, an improved follow-up MRI, a limited practice return — and then a plateau. Simmons goes on IR and misses at least 4 more games.

What fans heard along the way was different: team sources calling it a non-issue in August, week-to-week through September, "ramping up" on Monday, and a back/personal listing on Wednesday that the team never explained until the next day.

The context that made the silence costly: Simmons has played 8 career games and missed 4 games over a 22-day absence in the 2025 season that the team called a family situation. With that history, vagueness was never going to read as privacy — it read as desire. The Chiefs could have said in September what they said Thursday: he was in the building daily, rehabbing, and the question was leg strength. They chose not to, and he absorbed the blame for it.
