The [New England Patriots](/nfl/team/ne) are 6-0 in games immediately following a loss since Mike Vrabel took over in the 2025 season. New England has lost 7 games under Vrabel; 6 of them have had a next game, and New England won all 6.

That is not what usually happens after a loss. Since 1999, NFL teams win 45.4% of the game after a loss, 3010-3617, per nflverse. At that rate, winning 6 in a row happens about 0.9% of the time.

The wins came against the [Miami Dolphins](/nfl/team/mia), [Carolina Panthers](/nfl/team/car), [Baltimore Ravens](/nfl/team/bal), [Pittsburgh Steelers](/nfl/team/pit), [Buffalo Bills](/nfl/team/buf) and Miami, by an average of 10.7 points. The first 3 came in the 2025 season; the last 3 in the 2026 season:

Week 8 at MIA | 5

The streak is 6 games, and 4 of the 6 wins came by 6 points or fewer, so the streak has had its close calls. New England is 19-7 overall under Vrabel, 5-4 this season.

The next test comes Sunday against the [Detroit Lions](/nfl/team/det), 6-2, in Munich, a week after New England lost 27-45 to the [Green Bay Packers](/nfl/team/gb) in Week 9. 6 games is a small sample, and a streak this short can be luck as easily as coaching. What the numbers say is narrower: New England has answered every loss with a win under Vrabel, and the league usually does not.
