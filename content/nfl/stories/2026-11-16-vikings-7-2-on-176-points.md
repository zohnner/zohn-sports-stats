[Minnesota Vikings](/nfl/team/min) beat the [Green Bay Packers](/nfl/team/gb) 30-10 on Sunday, their largest win at Lambeau Field since 1999 — the previous best was a 16-point win — and moved to 7-2. The Vikings have scored 176 points in 9 games. Only 3 teams since 1999 have won 7 of their first 9 while scoring fewer: the 2001 Steelers with 155, the 2006 Broncos with 158, and the 2000 Giants with 168.

The record is a defensive product. Minnesota has allowed 126 points, the fewest in the NFL — 14.0 a game — and the next-fewest is Jacksonville at 144. 5 of the Vikings' 7 wins have come by 10 points or fewer, and their 2 losses came against Detroit (7-2) and Buffalo (8-1).

9 games is a small sample, and the offense has not had to win a shootout: Minnesota has not scored more than 30 since its 39-point opener. But a 7-2 team that allows 14.0 points a game is a real shape, not a standings quirk — and Sunday's 20-point win at Green Bay was the widest margin of the 7.
