Memphis beat No. 10 Louisville 98-78 on Sunday, the widest margin of any ranked team's loss to an unranked opponent this season. Ranked teams had lost only 2 times in 80 such games — Memphis 80-76 over No. 18 Iowa State, and LSU 81-80 over No. 14 Gonzaga on Saturday — and Memphis has now handed them 2 of their 3 losses. Ranked teams are 78-3 in 81 games against unranked opponents.

Jayden Reid had 24 points and 10 assists for Memphis, and Tylik Weeks scored 24. Memphis shot 58 percent from the field and 50 percent from 3-point range, on 10 of 20, and the 98 points were its most this season; Louisville had allowed 70 and 57 in its first 2 games. The Tigers led by as many as 20, and Louisville shot 7 of 29 from deep.
