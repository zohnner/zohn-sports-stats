**Title:** Villanova beat No. 18 Michigan 79-73; ranked teams are now 107-5 vs unranked

Villanova bounced back from an 81-61 loss to Pittsburgh on Tuesday to beat No. 18 Michigan 79-73 on Saturday. Tyler Perkins scored 23 points, and Trey McKenney had 22 for Michigan, including 5 3-pointers. Ranked teams are 107-5 against unranked opponents this season — Michigan's loss was the only one on Saturday, with No. 25 LSU, No. 2 Michigan State and No. 5 Duke all winning.
