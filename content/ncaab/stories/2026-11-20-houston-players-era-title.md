No. 8 Houston beat No. 11 Kansas 92-83 on Thursday to win the Players Era title in Las Vegas, its 3rd win in 3 days — and its 2nd over a top-11 team in that span. The Cougars are 6-0.

The run: Houston beat Rutgers 85-73 on Tuesday, beat No. 1 Florida 86-76 on Wednesday, and beat Kansas 92-83 in the final on Thursday.

Mercy Miller scored 21 points in the final, Gillespie added 19 and Dedan Thomas Jr. 16 for Houston; Stokes scored 22 for Kansas.
