[Colorado](/ncaab/standings) trailed Stanford by 20 points in the first half and won 70-65 at the Acrisure Series in Palm Springs on Tuesday. Stanford led by 20 late in the first half; Colorado trailed 38-24 at halftime and outscored Stanford 46-27 in the second half.

The second-half swing came in transition: Colorado scored 18 fast-break points to Stanford's 2. Barrington Hargress scored 19 points with 5 assists, and Justin Neely added 16 off the bench. Colorado's largest lead was 6 points, in the final minute.

Stanford shot 27 of 55 (49%) and scored 42 points in the paint, but managed 4 of 16 from 3-point range (25%). Julius Price led Stanford with 18 points. Stanford fell to 5-2; Colorado improved to 3-3.

No team on Tuesday overcame a larger halftime deficit than Colorado's 14 — Ole Miss matched it at BYU — and Colorado's deficit had reached 20 before halftime.
