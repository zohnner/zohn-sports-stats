Andrej Stojakovic scored a career-high 46 points as No. 10 Illinois beat No. 5 Duke 99-78 at Cameron Indoor Stadium on Tuesday, the most points ever by an opposing player in the building.

Stojakovic broke a record that had stood for 69 years, set by South Carolina's Grady Wallace with 43 points. He finished 14 of 21 from the field and 16 of 20 from the foul line, with 20 points by halftime, and came within 2 points of Dick Groat's 48 — the most by any player at Cameron.

The win ended Duke's 35-game home winning streak and was the Blue Devils' worst home loss in 37 years. Illinois led by as many as 23 and outrebounded Duke 54-34. Quentin Coleman scored 27, with 17 in the second half.
