No. 24 Kentucky beat winless Northern Arizona 78-77 on Friday, the closest call of the season for a ranked team. Across 80 games involving a ranked team since the season opened, it is the only one decided by 3 points or fewer. Ranked teams are 72-1 against unranked opponents; the one loss was Memphis over No. 18 Iowa State, 80-76.

The Lumberjacks did it from deep. Northern Arizona made 13 of 26 3-pointers, 50%, and Chris Komin scored 25 points on 5-of-8 shooting from beyond the arc, with 6 assists in 39 minutes. Komin outscored every Kentucky player by 11: Malachi Moreno, Milan Momcilovic and Alex Wilkins each scored 14, and Moreno added 6 rebounds and 6 assists.

Kentucky shot 56% from the field and turned it over just 4 times, but the Wildcats could not get another look: they grabbed 1 offensive rebound all night and were out-rebounded 32-20. Kentucky moved the ball, with 21 assists on 31 made field goals, yet Northern Arizona led for 41% of the game and the lead changed hands 13 times. Northern Arizona shot 52% from the field and 10 of 12 from the line. The Lumberjacks' largest lead was 5 points; Kentucky's was 13.
