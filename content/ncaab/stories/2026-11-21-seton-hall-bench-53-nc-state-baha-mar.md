Seton Hall's starters scored 34 points against NC State on Friday. Its bench scored 53 — and the Pirates needed all of them, beating NC State 87-81 in overtime to win the Baha Mar Championship in Nassau.

Rodney Brown Jr. scored 24 points off the bench on 7-of-11 shooting, and Terry Copeland Jr. added 22 on 8-of-9 shooting with 9 rebounds. The 2 reserves combined for 46 points, 12 more than the starting 5 managed.

The game was tied 73-73 after regulation, and Seton Hall won the overtime 14-8. NC State's Ven-Allen Lubin scored 27 points on 8-of-12 shooting and went 11 of 11 from the line.

Seton Hall led 30-29 at halftime, and the second half ended tied at 73-73 before the Pirates won the overtime. Elijah Fisher added 18 points for Seton Hall, and Brown played 36 minutes off the bench. The bench shot 17 of 29 from the field; the starters shot 12 of 26.

The title came a day after Seton Hall beat Wisconsin 74-69, so the Pirates won 2 games in 2 days over power-conference teams. Seton Hall is 5-1; its only loss came at No. 18 Alabama, 82-76.
