No. 14 Tennessee held No. 2 Michigan State to 9 made field goals in a 61-46 win on Tuesday, the first time in 77 years that an AP Top 20 team failed to reach 10 makes in a game.

Michigan State shot 9 of 50 from the field (18 percent), including 3 of 26 in the second half, the lowest field-goal percentage of Tom Izzo's 32 seasons as Michigan State's coach. The Spartans had averaged 93 points in their first 4 games and scored 46 — 47 below that average.

Jeremy Fears Jr. scored 16 points without making a field goal, going 16 of 18 from the foul line, and had 2 assists after entering the game averaging 10 a game. Tennessee shot 46 percent; DeWayne Brown II and Miles Rubin scored 14 each.
