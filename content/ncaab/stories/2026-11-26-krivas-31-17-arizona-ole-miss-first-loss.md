Motiejus Krivas scored 31 points and grabbed 17 rebounds as No. 8 Arizona beat Ole Miss 84-73 at the Lahaina Civic Center on Tuesday, handing the Rebels their first loss. Both numbers are season highs for Krivas, after 30 points against Cal Poly and 12 rebounds against Auburn.

Arizona trailed 40-33 at halftime and won the second half 51-33. Ole Miss led by as many as 13 points in the first half before Krivas took over inside: he shot 11 of 13 from the field and 9 of 14 from the free-throw line.

Ole Miss was 6-0 entering the game, having beaten BYU 73-63 the day before, and fell to 6-1. Arizona improved to 7-1, its only loss a 84-75 defeat to UCLA. Ivan Kharchenkov added 18 points for Arizona on 6-of-10 shooting, and Caleb Holt scored 14.

Arizona shot 58% from the field and outrebounded Ole Miss 39-27. Adam Clark led Ole Miss with 17 points.
