Indiana beat Kentucky 90-76 on Friday, and the Hoosiers are 6-0. The win came 2 days after Indiana beat No. 20 Arkansas 81-76. Kentucky was ranked No. 24 6 days earlier, so Indiana's last 2 wins both came against teams ranked in November.

Indiana has scored 90 or more in 4 of its 6 games — 91 against Eastern Illinois, 92 against Bellarmine, 90 against Tennessee Tech and 90 against Kentucky. The other 2 games were 83 against Syracuse and 81 against Arkansas.

The defense has held up too: Indiana has allowed 70.7 points a game, and no opponent has scored more than 80. Indiana is averaging 87.8 points a game, a 17.2-point gap between what it scores and what it allows.

Bryce Lindsay scored 20 points off the bench to lead Indiana, and Aiden Sherrell added 16. Kentucky's Milan Momcilovic scored 28, and 3 other Wildcats reached double figures, but Indiana's bench outscored Kentucky's 33-4. Kentucky scored 76 points, 12 fewer than the 88 it put up against Grambling 3 days earlier.
