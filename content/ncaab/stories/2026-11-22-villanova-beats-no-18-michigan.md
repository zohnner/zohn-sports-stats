Villanova beat No. 18 Michigan 79-73 on Saturday, 4 days after losing 81-61 to Pittsburgh on Tuesday. Tyler Perkins scored 23 points for the Wildcats, and Trey McKenney had 22 for Michigan, including 5 3-pointers.

Villanova led 34-29 at halftime and held on, winning the second half 45-44. Perkins went 10 of 13 from the free-throw line; Jake Fiegen added 15 points and Elijah Crawford 13. Roddy Gayle Jr. added 15 for Michigan.

Ranked teams are now 107-5 against unranked opponents this season, in 112 games. Michigan's loss was the only one on Saturday: No. 25 LSU beat Queens 106-75, No. 2 Michigan State beat Detroit Mercy 91-70, and No. 5 Duke beat Washington State 78-74.
