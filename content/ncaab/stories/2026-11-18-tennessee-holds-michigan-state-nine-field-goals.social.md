**Title:** Tennessee held No. 2 Michigan State to 9 field goals — a Top 20 low not seen in 77 years

No. 14 Tennessee beat No. 2 Michigan State 61-46, holding the Spartans to 9-of-50 shooting (18 percent), including 3-of-26 in the second half. It is the first time in 77 years an AP Top 20 team failed to reach 10 made field goals. Michigan State had averaged 93 points in its first 4 games; Jeremy Fears Jr. scored all 16 of his points from the foul line.
