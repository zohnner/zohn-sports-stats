LSU beat No. 14 Gonzaga 81-80 in Spokane on Saturday, ending the Bulldogs' 21-game home winning streak. Gonzaga had won all 19 of its home games last season and its first 2 at home this season; its last home loss was Feb. 23, 2025, 74-67 to Saint Mary's.

Donovan Dent led LSU with 23 points and 10 assists, and Abdi Bashir Jr. added 15 points on 5-of-8 shooting from deep. Braden Huff had 18 points and 13 rebounds for Gonzaga, and Davis Fogle added 15 points, 8 rebounds and 6 assists.

The loss is the 2nd by a ranked team to an unranked opponent this season. Ranked teams are 73-2 in 75 such games after starting 72-1 through Nov. 13; the first loss was Memphis 80-76 over No. 18 Iowa State on Nov. 2. LSU is 4-0, and the Gonzaga game was its first road game of the season.

It is early: LSU has played 4 games, and Gonzaga is 2-1 with a long season ahead. But a 21-game home streak takes more than a year to build, and LSU ended it in its first road game.
