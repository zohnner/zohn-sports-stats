No. 25 [Louisville](/ncaab/standings) beat No. 5 Texas Tech 72-64 at Michelob ULTRA Arena in Las Vegas on Tuesday, handing the Red Raiders their first loss of the season. Texas Tech was 5-0; Louisville improved to 4-1.

Seth Trimble scored a season-high 24 points off the bench on 7-of-13 shooting, including 3 of 4 from 3-point range. His previous high was 16 against Memphis. Louisville won the second half 46-39 after leading 26-25 at halftime.

Texas Tech shot 6 of 27 from 3-point range (22%) and managed 6 assists. Darrion Williams led the Red Raiders with 17 points. Texas Tech played without JT Toppin, who did not play. Louisville shot 21 of 23 from the free-throw line (91%) and outrebounded Texas Tech 41-28, including 14 offensive rebounds.

9 days after a 98-78 loss to Memphis, Louisville owns a top-5 win. Texas Tech fell to 5-1.
