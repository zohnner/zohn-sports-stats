No. 8 [Arizona](/ncaab/standings) got 86 of its 96 points from its starters in a 96-83 win over Colorado State at the Maui Invitational on Monday. All 5 starters reached double figures, and the bench scored 10 points.

Ivan Kharchenkov led Arizona with 24 points, Caleb Holt scored 20, Motiejus Krivas had 17, JJ Mandaquit had 15 and Derek Dixon had 10. The 5 starters combined for 86 points on 31-of-57 shooting.

Colorado State's starters scored 68 of its 83 points, with Carey Booth's 19 leading the Rams, but the Rams' bench scored 15.

Arizona led 50-34 at halftime and never trailed in the second half.

Arizona is 5-1. Colorado State fell to 4-2.
