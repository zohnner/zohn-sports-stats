John Blackwell scored 25 points as No. 16 Duke beat No. 10 UConn 77-63 at the T-Mobile Arena on Tuesday, holding the Huskies to their lowest point total of the season. Blackwell shot 7 of 11 from the field and 5 of 6 from 3-point range for a season high, after 17 against Michigan State.

Duke held UConn to 31% shooting and 11 of 37 from 3-point range, and led by as many as 15 points. UConn's 63 points are 16 below its previous low of 79 against Arizona. Duke shot 52% from the field, and Patrick Ngongba II added 17 points and 11 rebounds.

Duke is 5-2, with losses to Michigan State, 79-73, and Illinois, 99-78. UConn is 4-2, with losses to Arizona, 85-79, and Duke. Silas Demary Jr. led UConn with 25 points on 9-of-16 shooting, including 5 of 9 from 3-point range.
