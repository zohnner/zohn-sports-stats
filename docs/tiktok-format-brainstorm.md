# SportStrata TikTok — Format Brainstorm (2026-10-27)

*Working brainstorm, not a decision. Companion to the Pepper influencer build the same day — the question is what of that formula transfers to a serious data brand.*

## What actually worked in the Pepper formula

Strip the cat away and the machine is four parts:

1. **A recognizable host** the audience can identify in one second of scrolling.
2. **A fixed ritual** — same segment shape every video, ending in a verdict the audience waits for (Pepper's toe-bean rating).
3. **An interaction loop** — comments become the next video's material.
4. **A seamless pipeline** — finished, post-ready files; the human only picks material and posts.

## The translation problem

SportStrata's brand is Bloomberg-terminal serious: dense, fast, clean data, no hype. A mascot would dilute it. The Pepper lesson that transfers is not "get a character" — it's that **the host slot must be filled by something instantly recognizable**. For SportStrata, that slot is already built: **the site's own visual language** (scorebug, telemetry graphics, shot charts, the stories' stat cards). The data viz is the face.

The voice decision is the one real fork:

- **A. Synthetic "desk" voice** (TTS, same voice locked forever, like Pepper). Pros: fully automatable, daily cadence possible, no recording burden on a night-shift schedule. Cons: sports audiences reward a human voice; TTS analysis can feel soulless.
- **B. Zohn's own voice over the graphics.** Pros: trust, personality, matches what he was already doing with practice/interview clips. Cons: recording step breaks the seamless pipeline; cadence depends on his energy.

*Recommendation: launch with A for the daily data format (volume + consistency), use B for the bigger weekly pieces where his read is the value.*

## Format menu (ranked by ownability × safety)

### 1. Story-to-Video — the daily stories, performed (TOP PICK)
The daily stories pipeline already produces verified, data-only stories with `.facts.json` discipline and a `.social.md` per story. Each story is a finished script. The video: hook line → the chart/stat card animated on screen → verdict line → "full story at sportstrata.cc". Zero new research, zero footage rights issues (all graphics are ours), and it fixes the review's G1 finding (stories are orphaned — no distribution surface). **This is the Pepper pipeline with the story cron as the clip queue.**

### 2. Clip + The Data Says (Zohn's old format, formalized)
His previous approach — reporter-shot practice/interview clips (freely posted by teams/beat writers) with his analysis on top — maps exactly onto Pepper's structure: clip → host segment → verdict ritual. The upgrade vs. before: the analysis layer is a SportStrata stat card (what the data says about what the coach/player just claimed), karaoke captions, and a consistent end-verdict ("The data says: TRUE / NOISE / WAIT"). Rights posture: short clips, heavy transformation, credit the reporter/team in caption + on screen, instant takedown honor. Team-posted media and pressers are the safe end; **broadcast game footage stays off-limits** (league strikes are fast and real).

### 3. The Moment It Flipped (pure data viz, zero footage)
Win-probability swings, NBA shot-chart runs, a team's margin chart cratering — rendered from our own data, narrated by the desk voice. "This game was over at 8:47 of the 3rd. Here's the exact play the chart knew before the broadcast did." Nobody else posts this because nobody else has the site's telemetry layer. Safest format legally, most ownable creatively.

### 4. Comment-Reply Analytics (the interaction loop)
Fans argue in comments; the reply video settles it with data. "Was that luck?" → the numbers, 25 seconds, verdict stamp. Direct import of Pepper's comment engine, and every reply is also a soft ad for the site's depth.

### 5. Pick'em / Prediction content — KEEP OFF THIS ACCOUNT
Betting content lives in the PickSharp world. SportStrata stays the clean data brand (also keeps TikTok's gambling-content restrictions away from the account). One bright line, no exceptions.

## The ritual (Pepper's toe beans, translated)

Every video ends the same way: a verdict stamp in the site's visual language —
**"STRATA VERDICT: SIGNAL / NOISE"** for claims, or a single number ("the chart says 62%") for moments. The audience should be able to chant it. Consistency of the stamp is what turns clips into a show.

## Pipeline (mostly already built)

- **Script supply:** the daily stories cron (formats 1, 3) + a clip queue doc like Pepper's (format 2), vetted against the rights rules above.
- **Build:** the same builder pattern as Pepper — template segments, karaoke word captions, locked voice, burned-in credits, post-ready 9:16 MP4 + copy-paste caption. Zohn's job: pick + post.
- **Funnel:** every caption links the matching sportstrata.cc page (story URL, team page, leaders page). TikTok bio = the site. The videos exist to make the site's data famous, not to become a standalone media business (until/unless the numbers argue otherwise).
- **Cadence:** game-day-weighted. 1/day baseline from stories; clip formats on news days. Night-shift friendly because the pipeline is automated.

## What not to do

- No broadcast footage, ever.
- No injury/rumor content — same line as the stories playbook (D-166): data analysis only; a news event is a hook, never the body.
- No betting picks, odds, or PickSharp cross-promotion on this account.
- No mascot. The terminal aesthetic is the personality.

## Smallest experiment

Two weeks. Daily Story-to-Video (format 1) + two Clip + Data Says per week (format 2). Measure: median views, average watch %, profile taps → site clicks (UTM on the bio link), comments that ask a data question (the format-4 fuel). Kill/keep call at day 14 with real numbers, same as the Pepper pilot rules.
